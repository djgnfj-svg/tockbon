# 03-seams — lines between the 칸, and no fill at all

**What it buys** — **the ground keeps its own colour** and the board is merely ruled, so you can count
the 칸 and see exactly where a body will stand. It is the cheapest thing on screen and it reads at the
opening distance.

**What it costs** — a ruled board reads as a **boardgame**, not as an island; and the rule runs right
out to the shore, so the coast gets a second white line beside the one the sea already draws.

⚠ **What it CANNOT do** — **tell walkable from unwalkable.** The unreachable plateau is ruled exactly
like the ground under your feet, because a seam is about the grid and not about the rules.
