# 08-drift — the whole sea is one body of water going somewhere

**Where the open water comes from**: three travelling wavefronts at 17, 23 and 31 조각, on bearings a
few degrees apart, summed and read as depth. **A direction and a speed, not a field.** The mesh is
never touched — the sea stays flat.

- **What it buys** — **the water is legible as water rather than as background**, and it says so
  everywhere at once: it needs no island, no boat and no landmark. **It moves 81% of the `open` frame
  and 91% of `out`**, the widest reach in the sheet, at a maximum of 16 of 255 — so it fills the frame
  without ever being loud in any one place.
- **What it costs** — three sines per pixel, and **a bearing that is now a decision about the whole
  game's sea**: every island will have its swell running the same way, and the day the player sails,
  「with the swell」 and 「against it」 become visible facts.
- **⚠ What it CANNOT do** — **it cannot be judged from the sheet.** A still frame of a travelling
  swell is two soft light bands; everything this mechanism is for happens in the next second. **The
  three pictures show what it costs when nothing moves and not one thing it buys** — this row has to
  be run in the live lab or it is being judged on the wrong evidence. ⚠ And **the crests are what
  「자글자글」 would come back through**: they are 20-odd 조각 apart here and shortening them is exactly
  the move that killed the last round.
