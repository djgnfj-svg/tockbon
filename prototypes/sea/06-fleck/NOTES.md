# 06-fleck — things on the water you can count

**Where the open water comes from**: a lattice, one pale object per cell at a jittered spot with its
own size, the whole scatter drifting as one current. **Discrete, not a field.**

- **What it buys** — **scale, and a fixed thing to measure a crossing against.** The boat passes them;
  they do not pass with it. **Nothing else in the sheet gives the eye something it can point at**, and
  「자글자글」 is not available as a complaint about six objects.
- **What it costs** — nine hash lookups per pixel, and **two dials that fight**: `fleck_cell` decides
  how many are in frame and `fleck_r_max` decides how big, so 「more of them」 and 「bigger」 are not
  the same request. **It moves about 1% of the `open` frame and 2% of `out`** — six or seven objects
  near the island, about twelve out at sea.
- **⚠ What it CANNOT do** — **it cannot say anything about the water BETWEEN them.** Ninety-eight per
  cent of the sea is the shipped flat colour, untouched, and if the emptiness is the expanse rather
  than the lack of landmarks this mechanism has not addressed it at all. ⚠ **And they are unexplained
  objects**: nothing in the game says what a pale patch on the sea is, so they can read as dirt on the
  lens rather than as anything in the world.
