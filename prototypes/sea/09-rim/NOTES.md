# 09-rim — the sea has an edge, so it has a size

**Where the open water comes from**: how far out from the island's own middle a pixel is. The water
deepens over a long slow reach from 12 to 52 조각, and one pale stroke stands at 31. **The surface
itself is never touched.**

- **What it buys** — **extent.** A flat colour that continues identically forever gives a crossing
  nothing to be measured against; a boat that is further out is now visibly further out. **It moves
  63% of the `open` frame at a maximum of 12 of 255, and 79% of `out` at 32** — nearly all of what it
  does is out where the camera roams, which is the frame this round exists for.
- **What it costs** — one length per pixel, and **an anchor**: it reads the middle of the baked field,
  so it moves with the island rather than being written down. ⚠ **The two reaches are tied to the two
  cameras** — 31 is chosen so the stroke is absent from the opening frame and present in the roamed
  one, and a different island or a different roam moves both.
- **⚠ What it CANNOT do** — **it does nothing at all near the boat, and nothing at all in the middle
  distance.** Between the coast and 12 조각 out it is the shipped sea unchanged, which is most of the
  `open` frame and most of where the fighting happens. ⚠⚠ **And a circle around the island is a claim
  the game has not made**: nothing says the sea ends there, so the stroke can read as the edge of a
  map rather than as a horizon — and once the player sails, they will cross it.
