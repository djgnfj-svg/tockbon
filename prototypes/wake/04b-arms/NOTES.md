# 04b-arms — the two arms, and nothing inside them

**What it buys** — the V, and only the V. **About half the ink of `04a-full` with the whole shape
kept**: it still says which way the boat is going and exactly where it turned, and the water between the
arms is water again.

**What it costs** — the same 96 samples per pixel. **Dropping the crests buys nothing on the GPU** —
this is a cheaper picture, not cheaper code.

**What it CANNOT do** — **say how fast.** The crests are what read as motion; two bare lines are a
chevron, and a chevron looks the same behind a boat crawling and a boat running. ⚠ It also loses the one
part of the pattern that a player could learn to read as 「this one is coming in hard」.
