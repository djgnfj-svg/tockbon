# 04d-single — one line straight behind the boat

**What it buys** — **the simplest thing that still says a boat passed here.** One stroke down the track,
no V, no shape to get wrong, and it reads at every distance because there is only one thing to see. It
is also the only version whose meaning survives being drawn one pixel wide.

**What it costs** — the same 96 samples per pixel; the history is the whole mechanism and the line is
just a different thing drawn from it.

**What it CANNOT do** — **point.** A V has an apex and the apex is the boat; a line has two ends and
looks the same from either. ⚠ **With the hull off screen or hidden behind the island, this mark does not
say which end of it the boat is at**, and it says nothing about speed or about the size of what dragged
it. A rowboat and a warship leave the same stroke.
