# 04a-full — the whole shape, and the baseline the rest are read against

**What it buys** — **everything the mechanism can say at once**: the two 19.47° arms and the transverse
crests inside them. Which way the boat is going, where it turned, roughly how fast, and that what made
the mark was a hull and not a rock. **It is what the user picked off the first sheet**, with the arm
beading fixed and no other number moved.

**What it costs** — the busiest picture of the five, and the most white on the water at the frame the
game opens on. Every pixel of sea walks 96 samples, and the crests are the half of that which draws the
widest.

**What it CANNOT do** — **be quiet.** There is no camera distance at which this stops competing with the
island and the bodies for the eye. ⚠ **And the crest arcs sweep well outside the arms**, so two boats
landing near each other will lay their patterns across one another.
