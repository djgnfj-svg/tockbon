**What it buys** — **the resting board is untouched.** The 판 that ships stays exactly as baked and
this is a separate layer that can be deleted with no consequence to it. The cheapest thing to try, and
the cheapest to take back out.

**What it costs** — a second mesh of bars, one per seam, and **the bars are rectangles**: where two 판
meet near a coast a bar can reach past the land the 판 were carefully cut back from.

⚠ **What it CANNOT do** — **change the OUTER shape of a 칸.** The four 판 keep their own rounded outer
corners, so a merged 칸 reads as four rounded squares welded together rather than as one lump. **If the
answer is 「one 칸-sized 판」, this one cannot get there** — it can only fill what is between them.
