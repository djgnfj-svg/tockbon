# 02-ripple — the surface is bent and the sun finds it

**Where it comes from:** two long crossed sine trains tilt the water's normal. **The colour is never
touched** — every light and dark is the sun landing on a slope.

**What it buys** — **the quietest thing on the sheet that is still moving.** Broad soft bands, no edges
anywhere, nothing for the eye to catch on. It reads as water without ever becoming a pattern.

**What it costs** — two sines and a gradient a pixel. Nothing is built, nothing is baked.

**What it CANNOT do** — ⚠⚠ **it cannot be seen at a glance and it never will be.** A smooth lambert
swing on a smooth slope is a gradient, and a gradient on a large flat colour is the definition of
subtle. ⚠ 티켓 05 already reached this verdict once — *the four that only bent the normal could not be
told apart, they are all the same soft haze* — and doing it with real light rather than a painted
highlight did not change the verdict, it only made the haze honest.
