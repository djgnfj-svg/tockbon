# 02 · wood on earth · 1x2

**Planks laid on a bank of earth**: the ground is a plain slope and the steps are a thing somebody
carried there and dropped on top. 조각 둘, one wide.

- **What it buys** — it reads as BUILT rather than cut, which is what a fishing village has. Half the
  footprint of 01, and the width is one body, so the stair is the neck.
- **What it costs** — a wood tone the island's palette does not contain. On the sheet the planks come
  out nearly black against the grass, and darkening or lightening them is a round of its own.
- **What it CANNOT do** — carry two bodies abreast. At one 조각 wide the rule already refuses it, so a
  queue at the foot is permanent, not a tuning knob.

The mesh is `wood_1x2` in `../stairs.py`.
