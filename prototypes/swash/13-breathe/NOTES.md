# 13-breathe — The thickness swings from stretch to stretch

Pictures: `../out/island/13-breathe_*.png`

**The second reading of** 「얇다가 두꺼워지는」: the band is fat on one stretch of coast and thin on the
next, and which is which keeps changing.

**Buys** — a coast that is not uniform at any instant. ⚠⚠ **The phase is the one 08 already carried
down the tangent**, so the fat stretches DRIFT rather than pulsing in place — take that away and this
is a ring breathing, which is what the whole sea was rebuilt to escape on 2026-08-28.

**Costs** — one sine on a value that was already being read.

**Cannot** — ⚠ **be told apart from 12 in a still.** 12 is thin at the top of its run and fat at the
bottom, everywhere; 13 is uniformly thin HERE and uniformly fat THERE. **One frame of each can look
identical** — the difference is which way the thickness varies, and that only shows across the four
shots or watched.
