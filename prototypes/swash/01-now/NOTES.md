# 01-now — Noise at a world position (what the game ships)

Pictures: `../out/01-now_*.png`

**Buys** — four motions running out of step with each other, no gradient, one texture read, and a full
day of eye-tuning already spent on its forty dials. It never shears, never smears, and looks identical
from every camera angle.

**Costs** — nothing this lab can charge it. It is the cheapest of the nine.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **go anywhere.** The phase varies from spot to spot because it is read out of a noise
field at that spot, so each stretch is on its own clock and none of them is passing anything to its
neighbour. **A row of spots each blinking on its own is not a wave**, and no seed, scale or speed turns
one into the other. It also cannot know the island is there: the quiet stretches are quiet at random
and they drift, so the sheltered side is a different side every minute.
