# 03-swash — Lines come in, and the shore runs up and draws back

Pictures: `../out/03-swash_*.png`

**Buys** — water that ARRIVES. The distance to shore is a ready-made gradient; `fract` cuts repeating
lines out of it and subtracting time marches them landward, while a second wave on the same clock
slides the waterline itself up the rock and back. This is Cyanilux's shoreline breakdown stated as a
mechanism.

**Costs** — the incoming lines are 거품 under another name, and this island has now refused 거품 twice.
Everything downstream of this file inherits them.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **spend different amounts of time arriving and leaving.** A cosine is symmetric.
Cyanilux says so about this exact shader: *"it is not entirely accurate to real life, where the flow of
water up the shore tends to have a shorter duration than when it retreats, but I think it is good
enough."* It also moves only perpendicular to the coast — nothing here travels along it.
