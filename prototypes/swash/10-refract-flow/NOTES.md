**is not everywhere**, and that **goes away in places.** Every dial here that makes the shore more
even works against the thing that was chosen.

**Buys** — two unrelated kinds of absence, running at once:

- **06's refraction** thins the line to `bay_floor` inside a cove and keeps it thin, because a cove is

  wander off the bay it belongs to.
- **08's travel** crawls the pattern along the coast, so a thin stretch does not stay thin forever.
  **This is the「가끔씩」** — the now and then.

**Costs** — the most samples of any candidate that is not 07: two for the gradient, four for the
curvature, and everything the travel reads is read twice for the crossfade.

which 04 inherits and 06 sits on. **It is in the picture that was picked**, so it stays until it is
looked at on its own and rejected on its own — this island has removed 거품 twice, and neither time
was it wearing a refracted shoreline.
