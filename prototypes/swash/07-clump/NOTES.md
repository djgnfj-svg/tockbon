# 07-clump — The foam is cells, not a width

Pictures: `../out/07-clump_*.png`

**Buys** — an end to the traced-outline look. A thresholded distance is geometrically a perfect offset
curve, and an offset curve is the one shape water never makes. Cells that are born, spread and dissolve
break that up even when the line barely moves. Both stylised-water writeups found for this round build
their foam exactly this way, and Crest generates foam from shallow water on the same principle.

**Costs** — nine samples per pixel for the Voronoi, the most expensive of the nine by a wide margin.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **stop being a texture.** The sea this island keeps was rebuilt around one sentence —
*"expressing things in borders and not in… textures, not in field"* — and cells are field. **This
candidate argues against a decision that has already been made twice**, and it is here to be looked at
once rather than to win.
