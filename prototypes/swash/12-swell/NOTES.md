# 12-swell — The foam thickens as it lands

Pictures: `../out/island/12-swell_*.png`

**The first of two readings of** 「거품이 좀더 두깨가 얇다가 두꺼워지는 그런 흐림이 있었으면 좋겠고」 —
*"I would like the foam to have that blur where it is thin and then thickens"*.

**Here the thickness depends on WHERE the foam is**: thin where it is born, fattest against the stone.
13 is the other reading — thickness by which stretch of coast, swinging in time.

**Buys** — the physical version. A wave running up rock piles its own water against the stone, so the
sheet is thinnest while it still has somewhere to go. ⚠ It also puts the widest part of the foam right
beside the shoreline, which answers 「거리가 멀어」 a second time.

**Costs** — one mix. It is the cheapest of the four.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **be pushed past about `near_w` 2.5 without the foam and the shoreline becoming one
band.** They are supposed to read as two things a small distance apart; merged, the gap stops being a
gap and becomes blur.
