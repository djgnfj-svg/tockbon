# 23-twoline - two whites, which is what the reference has

Pictures: `../out/island/23-twoline_*.png`

**Three changes, all from one message** (2026-08-29, the user: the swell comes too often and should be
slower, with two reference frames, and the line could be thinner still).

⚠⚠ **HARDNESS, NOT WIDTH, IS WHAT MAKES A LINE READ AS THIN.** `line_hard` went 0.45 -> 0.85. A soft
edge spends most of its width on a fade, so it looks thick however narrow it is set; the reference
frames have a hard stroke on a dark ground. The width came down too (0.045 -> 0.035 tiles) but that
only became legible once the edge was hard.

22 plus a second crisp stroke standing about a fifth of a tile off the rock, with dark water between
the two.

⚠⚠ **THE SECOND WHITE IS A LINE, NOT A BAND.** Every foam this lab drew before this was a soft wash
fading outward; the reference frames have a stroke. **That is why they read as thin and busy at the
same time** - two hard marks and a gap, rather than one gradient.

⚠ The gap opens as the water comes up, so the second line breathes rather than travelling.
