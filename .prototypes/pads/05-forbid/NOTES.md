# 05-forbid — the walkable ground is left bare and the rest is dressed

**What it buys** — the ground you may walk on stays **exactly as it was painted**, and the two places
no body can reach are unmistakable from the opening distance. No key, no selection, nothing to hold.
Bad North shipped this one: forests on the ground you cannot use.

**What it costs** — the art has to carry the covering (forest, scrub, rock), and the claim it makes is
computed once — a stair built later changes what is forbidden, so the dressing has to be rebuilt with
the island.

⚠ **What it CANNOT do** — **mark the 칸 under the cursor, or where one body may go.** It is a statement
about the map and never about a body. On an island where everything is reachable it draws nothing at
all.
