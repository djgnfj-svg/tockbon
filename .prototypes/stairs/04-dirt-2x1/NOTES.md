# 04 · earth ramp · 2x1

**One 조각 deep and two wide: a metre across and a metre up, so it is 45 degrees.** No treads at all —
the ground was cut away and left as a slope. 조각 둘, two wide.

- **What it buys** — the smallest way up that still carries two bodies abreast, and the cheapest thing
  to build: one sloped face.
- **What it costs** — at 45 degrees it is steep enough that a body's feet climb faster than anything
  else on the row, and the stepped foot heights have nothing under them to land on.
- **What it CANNOT do** — read as 「the only way up」. It is a triangle of the same grass as the floor,
  and 티켓 06 exists because the way up has to be legible from the picture alone.

The mesh is `dirt_2x1` in `../stairs.py`.
