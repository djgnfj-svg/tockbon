# 05 · grass hill · 2x2

**A whole 블록 given over to a bank of turf.** The ground swells up to the storey on a curve and the
sides fall away, so there is no built object anywhere. 조각 넷.

- **What it buys** — nothing was made, so nothing can look out of place. It is the only candidate that
  would still read on an island with no people on it.
- **What it costs** — 조각 넷, and the bank is so gentle that from the game camera it is a shadow
  rather than a shape.
- **What it CANNOT do** — be found. A player looking for the way up has to already know where it is,
  which is the opposite of what the ticket asks for.

The mesh is `hill_2x2` in `../stairs.py`.
