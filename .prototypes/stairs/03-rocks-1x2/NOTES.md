# 03 · rock ledges · 1x2

**Three boulders a body scrambles over.** Nothing was built: the way up is that the rock happens to
break in three. 조각 둘, one wide.

- **What it buys** — no built object at all, so it can stand anywhere on a wild coast. Same footprint
  as 02.
- **What it costs** — the three lumps read as white crates from above; at play distance the eye does
  not separate them from the storey's own wall.
- **What it CANNOT do** — say which way it climbs. A stair's picture carries its direction; three
  boulders do not, and the mouth rule then disagrees with what is on screen.

The mesh is `rocks_1x2` in `../stairs.py`.
