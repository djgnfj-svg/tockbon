a soft threshold. **A fine texture everywhere** — what `01-crests`, `02-facets` and `05-paper` all

  measured on 2026-08-29: the user's objection was to the SCALE and to what it does 「멀리서 봤을때」,

  wins, the finding is that the last round was wrong about something — not that this shader should be
