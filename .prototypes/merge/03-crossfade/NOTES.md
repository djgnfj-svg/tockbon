# 03-crossfade — two finished boards, and the camera picks one

**What it buys** — **both ends are exactly what you want and neither is a compromise.** The merged
board is authored separately, so it can be any shape at all — a whole-island wash, a 칸 lump, anything —
and it owes nothing to how the resting board is built.

**What it costs** — **two boards in memory, both drawn while crossing.** ⚠ **In the middle they are
both half-transparent and the island reads as a double exposure** — it is plainly visible in the `mid`
shot, and it is not a tuning problem: it is what fading between two overlapping boards looks like.

⚠ **What it CANNOT do** — **be crossed slowly.** There is no in-between shape, only two shapes at half
alpha, so every distance where both are visible is a distance where the board looks wrong. It wants a
hard switch, and a hard switch is a moment the eye catches.
