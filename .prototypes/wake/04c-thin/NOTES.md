# 04c-thin — the arms, thinner and shorter

**What it buys** — **the quietest of the shaped versions.** A hairline V that dies in half the time, so
the water two boat-lengths astern is clean and the mark stays close to the hull it belongs to. Four
numbers apart from `04b-arms`: width, edge hardness, life, opacity.

**What it costs** — the same 96 samples per pixel again. ⚠ **And a hard thin stroke is the one most
exposed to aliasing** — it is a sub-pixel line at any distance, so it will crawl when the camera turns
or zooms in a way the soft wide versions will not.

**What it CANNOT do** — **be seen at the frame the game opens on.** In `04c-thin_far.png` it is very
nearly gone. That is the picture and not an opinion: **this version is a close-camera answer**, and the
landing is watched from the far one.
