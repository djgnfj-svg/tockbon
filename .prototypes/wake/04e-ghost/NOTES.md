# 04e-ghost — the whole shape, much fainter

**What it buys** — **simplicity by loudness rather than by subtraction.** Arms and crests are both still
there, so everything `04a-full` says is still on screen for anyone who looks at it; it simply stops
being the loudest thing in the frame. **One number differs from `04a-full`** — the opacity.

**What it costs** — exactly what `04a-full` costs. Same samples, same shape, same everything.

**What it CANNOT do** — **hold up against anything brighter than this sea.** It was chosen over flat
open water with nothing else in the frame; put the island's white 해안선 or a lit body beside it and it
may fall under what registers at all. ⚠ **And at the far camera it is close to nothing** — a wake that
has to be looked for is a wake that will be reported as missing.
