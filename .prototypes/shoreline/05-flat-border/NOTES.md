# 05-flat-border — A border and nothing else (Bad North's own answer)

Picture: `../out/05-flat-border.png`

**Buys** — almost nothing to run: one texture read, no lighting, no ripple, no swell. It is the bar this project set itself, stated by Bad North's designer as **「expressing things in borders and not in field」**, and it is the cheapest thing on this sheet by a wide margin.

**Costs** — the field, same as 01.

✅ **CHOSEN 2026-08-28**, and then given compound movement on 「단순하게 원이 아니라 이제 복합적으로
움직이는걸 원한 진짜 물처럼」. **Four motions run at once and none of them is in step with the others**:
three octaves bending the line itself at three sizes and three drifts, a thickness that reads its phase
ALONG the coast so one stretch runs up while the next draws back, a line that lets go once a cycle over
patches only, and a slow patch map that leaves some stretches nearly still. ⚠ **Take any one out and it
goes back to a ring** — a border on one clock pulses, and that is the thing the user was pointing at.

**Cannot** — do anything but the line. No shallows, no depth, no movement, no reaction to a boat. ⚠ **It is on the sheet as the FLOOR**: if it holds up beside the other four, everything above it is being paid for without being needed.
