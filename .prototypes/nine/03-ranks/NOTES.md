# 03-ranks — the SQUAD owns the seat, and the squad faces something

Three ranks of three, turned to the facing. Shoulder to shoulder across (2/3 조각), tighter front to
back (0.5 조각), because that is what a line of men looks like.

- **What it buys** — **the picture says which way the nine are pointed.** It is the only version that
  carries information the others do not, and it is the shape Bad North's squads read as.
- **What it costs** — the front-to-back pitch is tighter than the across pitch, so **the middle rank is
  half hidden behind the front one** at this camera. Widening it to 2/3 makes this version identical to
  `02-grid` at facing south, which is to say the whole difference lives in that one number.
- **What it CANNOT do** — say what happens when two squads share a 블록 facing different ways. Every
  other version computes a seat from the 블록 alone; this one needs a fourth input, and two squads with
  different facings would interleave with nothing deciding who gets which place.
