# 04-stagger — the 블록 owns the seat, and every other row shifts half a place

Rows nest into the gaps of the row below, so the row pitch is √3/2 of the across pitch instead of 1.

- **What it buys** — **the nine take less depth than a square grid**, which at this camera pitch is the
  axis that costs the most: the board is seen from above and behind, so depth is the direction bodies
  hide each other in.
- **What it costs** — it is **harder to count**. Nothing lines up, so the eye has no row to run along,
  and at x1.25 the middle row's shoulders disappear into both neighbours.
- **What it CANNOT do** — present a front. The three rows never read as three ranks, so this is right
  for a mob and wrong for men under orders — and the nine here are men under orders.
