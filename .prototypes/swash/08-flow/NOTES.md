# 08-flow — The same travel as 02, without the smear

Pictures: `../out/08-flow_*.png`

**Buys** — 02's crawl with a bound on it. Two copies of the pattern, each dragged for half a cycle and
then restarted, weighted so whichever is freshest is the one on screen. The seam is spread evenly over
the surface instead of piling up at the corners. This is the standard flow-map fix, and it is what
Waterways bakes into a river.

**Costs** — everything 02 samples, twice. Plus one dial (`cycle`) that trades shear against a visible
throb.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **ever be perfectly sharp.** Two copies crossfaded are two copies crossfaded: there is
a permanent, low-level softness that no setting removes, and it lands on the same thin white line the
whole sea is made of. **Judge it against 02 at the point and nowhere else** — on a straight stretch 02
is sharper and identical in every other way.
