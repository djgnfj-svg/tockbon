# 16-rest — The resting line and the surge are two separate widths

Pictures: `../out/island/16-rest_*.png`

**The structural fix for** 「기본값이 너무 있어」 — *"the base value is too much"* (2026-08-29, the
user, on 11: 「얇게 있다가 파형이 왔을떄만 두꺼워야하는데 미리 흔적이 너무 남아있는느낌이야」).

⚠⚠ **11 COULD NOT HAVE BEEN TUNED INTO THIS.** It computed one width and let everything modulate it,
so the refraction, the drain and the incoming foam were all scaling a band that was already at full
thickness before any wave arrived. **Its drain term made the line WIDER while the water was leaving**,
which is the opposite of a trace fading.

**Buys** — a hairline that does nothing, plus width the wave adds on top of it. `up` is at zero for
most of the cycle, so **most of the time the coast is a hairline and nothing else.**

⚠ **The incoming foam rides `up` as well now.** In 11 it ran on its own clock and was on screen whether
or not water was up — that is most of what 「미리 흔적」 was.

**Costs** — one more dial than 11 (`rest_frac`), and the `count` correction that 11 needed anyway.

**Cannot** — **be read in a single frame.** Caught at the top of a run it is 11; caught between runs it
is a hairline. **The whole claim is about which of those is rare**, so it is judged watched or across
the four shots.
