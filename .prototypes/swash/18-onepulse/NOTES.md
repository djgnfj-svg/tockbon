# 18-onepulse — There is no separate foam. The surge IS the line

Pictures: `../out/island/18-onepulse_*.png`

**The most literal reading of** 「얇게 있다가 파형이 왔을떄만 두꺼워야하는데」 — *"it should be thin and
only thicken when the wave arrives"*. A hairline on the rock; when a wave runs up, that same line
swells and goes back. **Nothing else is drawn at all.**

⚠⚠ **THE INCOMING BANDS ARE DELETED, NOT DAMPED.** 03's `fract` train — inherited by every candidate
from 04 through 17 — is not in this file, and `reach`, `count` and `wave_w` went with it. **That train
is 거품**, which this island has removed twice; 16 and 17 keep it only because it was in the picture
that was praised on the round before.
⇒ **If 18 is enough, 거품 has been rejected a third time and the sea is one line that breathes.**

**Buys** — the fewest moving parts of anything built in this lab, and the thinnest resting coast.

**Costs** — the least of the eighteen. There is no second thing to draw.

**Cannot** — **show water arriving from anywhere.** A line that swells in place says the sea rose; it
does not say a wave came in from the west. ⚠ **If the island needs to feel like it is being reached,
this is the candidate that cannot do it** — that is what the train was for.
