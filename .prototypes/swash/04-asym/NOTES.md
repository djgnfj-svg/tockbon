# 04-asym — It rushes up and drains back, and those take different lengths of time

Pictures: `../out/04-asym_*.png`

**Buys** — the one asymmetry the eye can name without knowing it is naming it. The water is up the rock
in a fifth of the cycle and spends the other four fifths draining, and while draining the line is drawn
wider and fainter — the thinning film water leaves behind it on stone.

**Costs** — one branch and one more dial (`rise_frac`) than 03. Nothing else.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **be seen in a still picture.** Every frame of this is a frame 03 also produces; the
whole difference is which frames are common and which are rare, so **it can only be judged watched, or
across the four shots.** A reviewer handed one PNG will call it identical to 03 and be right about
the PNG.
