# 15-all — 12, 13 and 14 together

Pictures: `../out/island/15-all_*.png`

**Buys** — the answer to whether three sources of absence read as one sea, which cannot be had by
reasoning about three shaders separately.

**Costs** — everything above, added up.

**Cannot** — ⚠⚠ **stop the three from multiplying.** A stretch that is far out (12 says thin), on the
thin half of its swing (13 says thin) and behind a closing gate (14 says none) gets all three at once,
and the clamp at the bottom is the only thing between this and a flickering band.

⚠ **If it reads as three unrelated things fighting over one white line, the answer is not to tune this
file — it is to pick two of the three.**
