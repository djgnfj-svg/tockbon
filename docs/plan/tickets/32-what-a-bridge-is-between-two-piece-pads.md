# What says 「you may GO from here to there」 now that the 판 is a 조각

## The question

**Between two 조각 판, what replaces the bridge?**

## Why this is asked

**A 판 used to say only 「you may STAND here」 and that was ambiguous** (2026-08-28, the user, on the game
screen: 「조각이 이 층 조각이 조금 애매한 거 그 판정이 애매해 ... 걸쳐져 있다 못 가는 부분이 확실히
되는데 그런 게 좀 안 돼 있는데」). A low 판 and a high 판 sat side by side with a wall between them and
nothing on screen said so: the gap between two that CONNECT looked exactly like the gap between two that
do not.

⇒ **A sloped strip — a bridge — was drawn between every pair of 칸 a body could actually cross between**,
and the user kept it on sight (「경로선이 보이는건 좋은데」).

⚠⚠ **The bridges went out with the 칸 판 on 2026-08-29 and nothing replaced them.** The board says
「stand」 and says nothing about 「go」.

## What has to be decided

- **A strip on every neighbouring pair welds the board into one slab.** With 284 판 and every flat
  neighbour crossable, a bridge between each is a poured floor — which is what the gutters exist to stop
- **The case it was written for is the LEVEL CHANGE**, not the flat one: the way up, and the way down.
  ⚠ **There is no stair on the island right now**, so there is nothing on screen to judge a bridge
  against
- **Whether the answer is a strip at all.** The shape could be the 판's own edge instead — two 판 that
  connect could face each other flat, and two that do not could turn a rounded corner away

⚠ **Blocked on the stair** (ticket 06). Guessing at it before there is a level change to look at puts a
guess on screen before anyone can see it was one.
