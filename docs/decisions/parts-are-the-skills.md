# A body part *is* the skill; two of them get bound to the mouse

**Status**: valid

## What was decided

There is no skill list. A part equipped in one of the eight slots is a passive; **binding it to left click,
right click or space** makes it an active. Space accepts movement parts only (hindlimbs, back).

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| A separate skill/ability roster alongside parts | Adds a fourth noun and a second art pipeline for the same fantasy |
| The species fixes the skill (crocodile ⇒ bite, always) | Kills combination — the reason parts exist at all |
| Everything equipped is usable | With eight slots there is no choosing, and "what do I hold" is a free decision the design wanted |

## What's tied to it

Slot count (8), the eat-only starting state, and the claim that movement parts are the strongest hunting
motive — that only holds while space is a scarce, bindable slot.

## Conditions to reopen

If three bindings turn out to be too few to express a build, the honest fix is more bindings, not a
separate skill system.
