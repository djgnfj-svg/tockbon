# The player does not leave the core to fight — melee is cut as a feature

**Status**: valid

## What was decided

`next-game.md` had a chain: a ranged monster shoots a planted circle → the player walks out and kills it →
"where should I be standing" becomes the question every second. **That whole chain is cut**, and with it the
player-side melee combat it existed to justify.

The reason is not that the chain is bad. It is that **it makes the player the answer to every threat**, which
turns a defense game into an action game with turrets attached — two systems to build in twelve weeks, and
the circles stop being the thing the run is about.

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Player melee combat (going out and killing the shooter) | Two deep systems instead of one. The December date allows one |
| "Where should I be standing" as the core question | It only works if leaving is possible. Cutting the leaving kills the question |
| Ranged monsters as the thing that forces the player out | The monsters may stay; **what they force is now open** and belongs to the circles, not the player |

## What's tied to it

- **The user's melee taste, which was one of the two reasons the old game died.** That taste is no longer
  served by the player's hands. Whatever replaces it has to satisfy it some other way, or the same failure
  is being re-run with different art
- `next-game.md`'s "Why ranged monsters are load-bearing" section — its stated reason is now void
- The carried-vs-planted choice. Carrying a circle was partly about following the player *out*

## Conditions to reopen

The replacement dopamine moment turns out to be something the player's hands have to perform, and standing
still cannot perform it.
