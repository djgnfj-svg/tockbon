# Terrain goes in, nothing gets deferred, and the schedule takes the hit

**Status**: valid — decided by the user 2026-08-14, twice, after being offered the cut both times.

## What was decided

**Rocks and water pools both ship.** Rocks block — they are the wall that makes herding work inside the
field instead of only at its edges. Water **slows anything in it and hides it from critters**.

**Nothing is deferred to make room.** The scope that grew today — terrain, herding, the crow's counter-attack,
the arena — all of it stays in August. **The schedule is what gives.**

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| **Dropping the water pools, keeping rocks** | Offered as the cheapest cut, since herding needs blocking and water does not block. Refused |
| **Dropping parts and cards, keeping the hunt** | Offered. Refused |
| **Water that only slows** | The user added hiding on top: it is the ambush half of a stage otherwise built on being seen |
| **Water that clones cannot cross** | Splitting the swarm on terrain is a second mechanic wearing the same colour |

## What's tied to it

- **`grassland-field` was already the largest of the four plans and this makes it larger.** It was judged
  NOT BUILDABLE before terrain was added — see the 2026-08-14 adversarial review
- **Two walls, two opposites.** Rocks stop the horse; water stops nothing but lets you disappear.
  A hunt has a place to corner and a place to wait
- **This is a date decision, not a design one.** "August" now means whenever it is done

## Conditions to reopen

**The user names a date they need it by.** Then the cuts above are the list, in that order.
