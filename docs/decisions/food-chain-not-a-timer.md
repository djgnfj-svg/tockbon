# The run is a climb up the food chain, not a survival timer

**Status**: **partly reversed (2026-08-13)** by
[A stage is a habitat you clear by swallowing its boss](ladder-of-habitats-not-tiers.md)

⚠ **What survives**: the run is a climb, not a survival timer, and enemies are chimeras built from the
player's own slots. **What is dead**: the five-minute boss cadence, the tier abstraction, and the
animals → humans → space ladder. A stage is now a **habitat**, cleared by swallowing its boss, and the
ladder is beasts by habitat → dinosaurs → a final boss on its own stage. **The rejected-branch table below
is still the record of why region conquest lost the first time** — it lost to "territory needs a map
system", and that objection died when the map became one habitat-sized field per stage.

## What was decided

**Small animals → large animals → humans and their war machines → space.** Every five minutes a boss
**comes hunting for the player**; killing it in its arena opens the next tier. Bosses and monsters are
chimeras assembled from the same eight slots the player uses.

Opening a tier **adds** to the field rather than replacing it, and the field carries two tiers at a time.
The GDD holds the rule.

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Survive N minutes, then a boss (survivors standard) | Cheapest to build, but "king of the species" has no meaning in it and the growth is a number, not a picture |
| Conquer map regions one at a time | Territory needs a map system the game does not otherwise need |
| Defend against waves | The defense direction was shelved whole on 2026-08-12; not reopened |
| Purpose-built monster designs | Chimeras reuse the player's part system, so enemy art and enemy code cost nearly nothing |
| Starting the climb at microbes | A microbe has no part to give, so the opening minutes hand out nothing — and on minimal art it is a dot that reads as nothing. **The run starts at animals**, and the ladder runs animals → humans → space |

## What's tied to it

The one moment the whole run is built around: **what hunted you last tier is food in the next one.** Take
the tiers away and that reversal has nowhere to happen.

## Conditions to reopen

If a five-minute boss cadence makes tiers feel like a timer with extra steps, the cadence moves before the
structure does.
