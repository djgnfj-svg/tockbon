# Clones are stupid by default, and intelligence is bought

**Status**: valid

## What was decided

A scattered clone eats and fights and nothing else — it does not flee, does not notice danger, and dies
standing still. Intelligence is a **part** (the eyes slot), so a clone can grow out of it by hunting.

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Clones avoid danger on their own | Comfortable, and it removes the player's reason to watch the swarm at all — planning principle 1 |
| Clones run home when threatened | The middle option. Mistakes stop hurting, and so does the tension |
| Clone AI as a permanent between-run upgrade | Would make it a stat boost, which the meta layer explicitly does not do |

## What's tied to it

The entire answer to "what are the hands doing while the swarm feeds itself". `follow` being a rescue
button, not just a harvest button, depends on clones being unable to save themselves.

## Conditions to reopen

If watching dumb clones die reads as unfair rather than as the player's mistake.
