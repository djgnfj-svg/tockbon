# Breath gets its own slot — eleven, not ten

**Status**: valid — decided by the user 2026-08-14. **Partly reverses**
[Ten slots, and no internal slot duplicates another](ten-slots-no-duplicates.md), which cut lungs by name.

## What was decided

**The internal slots are five: eyes · gut · bone · hide · lung.** Lung is **breath** — how long a movement
active sustains before speed drops back to base.

It came up because the horse's three parts are legs, mane and **lungs**, and the settled ten had no square
for breath. Put to the user, the cheap answer was refused.

## What wasn't chosen

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| **Folding breath into `gut`** | The user's answer was direct: **eyes and gut both keep their own jobs.** Gut is how much a clone brings home — an economy number — and breath is a movement number. Sharing a square would have made one of them invisible |
| Dropping the horse's lungs and shipping two horse parts | Legs without breath is a gallop with no ceiling, and the three-part set is what makes going all-in on the horse readable |
| Making breath a property of the legs part | Then one card carries the whole movement build and there is nothing to collect |
| Staying at ten by cutting `hide` | Hide and torso were split on purpose — being seen and being thick are two different things. That fork is in the doc this one amends |

## What's tied to it

- **The count itself, which has now moved five times** — eight, twelve, nine, ten, eleven — **without
  anything being built on it.** That is the argument for the number living in exactly one place. It is in
  `stages-and-evolution`'s *Eleven slots* and in the plan's slot table, and nowhere else
- **The horse trait**, which fires on all three horse parts
- **`space`**, which only accepts movement actives — breath is what makes that key have a resource

## Conditions to reopen

**If a second breath-shaped part never appears.** A slot that exactly one part in the game ever fills is a
property wearing a slot's clothes, and folding it into `gut` becomes right again.
