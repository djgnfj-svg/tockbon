# What you carry into planning

**The standard by which a direction is judged.** It does not change when the direction does.
**One line each. Keep it one line each when adding.**

---

## 1. The hand must not idle

If there is time spent watching something roll on by itself, the game is stifling. **This one line is why a
whole tower-defence direction was folded**, and squads are commanded on the board at any time because of it.
⚠ If the fight ever feels like dead air, this is the first line to re-read.

## 2. Planning cannot decide whether something is fun

A day of changing direction five times answered nothing that one multi-day prototype closed the same day.

## 3. Never having finished one is the biggest hole right now

Every game has a stretch that looks unfun, and **only someone who has finished one knows it passes.**
The next one goes to the end even if it is not fun.

## 4. Four nouns and the game will not draw

**There are two systems.** One way of fighting, one way of building.

## 5. If it does not reduce to one sentence, it does not sell

If the pitch needs two sentences, it has already lost.

## 6. What sells is a single screenshot

Games sell because **one frame shows something insane.** A plan whose one frame does not draw needs another
look.

## 7. Pick the direction that does not get stuck on art

The direction that needs fewer assets wins. If one of them works with a single circle, that is the one.

## 8. A combination must have its result changed by order

A straight power-up is not a combination. **Order changing the KIND of result** is the bar — seen on screen
once, in a deleted game, and never since.
