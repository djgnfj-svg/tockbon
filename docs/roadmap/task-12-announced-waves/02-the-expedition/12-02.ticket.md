## ✅ What is decided

- **The home island keeps running while the player is away** — 「섬은 섬대로 또 움직이는 거임」
- **Both boards can be looked at**, one key between them — 「볼 수 있지」
- **The boat takes time both ways** — 「배가 오가는데 시간이 걸리지?」
- **What is left at home defends itself.** ⚠ **Already built**: a 검사 notices a 늑대 at 3.0 조각 and
  fights with no order (07-01, 2026-09-02). The user raised a turret and set it down on hearing this
- **What is brought back is material** — 짐승의 뼈와 가죽, and ore that the home island does not have
- **The home island is what can be lost**, which is why defending it survives at all: the answer to
  「why not drop the base and make it an adventure」 was that what is carried home is only usable there

## ⚠ What is NOT decided — **the user answers these before anything is built**

**How long the crossing takes**, and whether it is the same both ways.
**What happens if the alarm rings and the player cannot get back in time** — the wave lands on
whoever stayed, and nothing says what losing the 성채 while away means.
**How many bodies may go**, and whether one boat is the cap.
**Whether the expedition island is generated per trip or picked from a few.**
**Whether the alarm can ring while the player is mid-fight on the other island.**
