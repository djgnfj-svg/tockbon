### The shape in `battle.gd` — ⚠⚠ **the landing clock and the launch queue are SEPARATE**

**This is the first draft's worst defect and the reason it is worth writing down.** The draft advanced
one counter on launch and read the warning off it, so **wave 1 — a single boat — closed its own warning
the moment its hull was born, and the countdown vanished at 18 s instead of reaching 0.** In the other
direction the warning had no floor, so a wave whose last hull launches after its own minute left a
**negative countdown on screen for up to 31.75 s** (wave 7). ⇒ **Two clocks, not one counter.**

**The landing clock — what the screen reads.**

- **`wave_ordinal`** — which wave lands NEXT, counted from 0. **It advances when `elapsed` reaches the
  landing time, never when a hull is born**
- **`wave_land_sec`** — `WAVE_FIRST_SEC + wave_ordinal * WAVE_INTERVAL_SEC`
- **`wave_seconds_left`** — `max(0.0, wave_land_sec - elapsed)`. ⚠ **The `max` is the floor** that keeps
  a negative countdown off the screen
- **`wave_warning_open`** — `wave_seconds_left <= WAVE_WARNING_SEC`. It opens at 5:00, falls 180 → 0,
  and closes at 8:00 in the same sub-step `wave_ordinal` advances and `wave_seconds_left` jumps to 480

**The launch queue — what puts hulls in the water.**

- The row is `min(wave_ordinal, WAVE_BOATS.size() - 1)` — **this is what repeats the seventh row**
- When `elapsed` reaches `wave_land_sec - BOAT_CROSSING_SEC` and this wave has not been scheduled,
  push its hulls' launch times: that moment plus `k * gap` for each `k`
- Each sub-step, pop every entry now due and launch it, **gated by `boats_come`**
- ⚠ **The queue outlives `wave_ordinal` advancing** — wave 7's last hull is born 31.75 s after its own
  minute. **That is correct and is why the two clocks are separate.** At 8-minute intervals two waves'
  queues can never overlap

⚠⚠ **`Battle.setup` MUST reset `wave_ordinal`, the scheduled-wave mark and the queue.** `battle.gd`
already resets `_beach_cursor` and `_boats_launched` there and writes out at length why. **`Run` makes a
fresh `Battle` per island so the game would not show this** — the contract 「a reused `Battle` is
indistinguishable from a new one」 is what breaks, silently, exactly where nobody looks.

⚠⚠ **It rides the existing `elapsed` and the existing 60 Hz sub-step, and adds no second real clock.**
`how-nets-lie` opens on the deleted game's three clocks and the five defects that came out of the seam
between them.

⚠ **`boats_come` stays as a field.** Only the shell line that sets it false is deleted. `net_boats`
drives both arms of it and removing the field is scope this ticket was not given.
