## What this ticket builds

**A warning that goes up before every wave, the first one included: what is coming, and how long is
left.** Until it rings the player is free to be somewhere else.

### Where it came from

> The user, 2026-09-02: *"Before a wave comes, give a clear signal. So the time left can be spent
> outside farming."* (「웨이브가 오기 전에 확실한 신호를 주자. 그래서 남은 시간 동안 나가서 파밍할 수
> 있도록」)
>
> And on the first wave: *"Not wolves just turning up out of nowhere at the start either — you are
> farming, and it says a pack of wolves arrives in one minute."* (「처음에도 무작정 늑대가 오는 게 아니라
> 처음에 파밍하다가 일 분 뒤 늑대의 무리가 옵니다 하면 되잖아」)

## ✅ What is decided

- **Every wave is announced, the first included** — there is no unannounced attack
- **The announcement carries a clock** — 「in five minutes」, 「in one minute」 were both said
- **It names what is coming** — 바이킹, 늑대 무리. Not just 「something」
- **It is heard, not found.** ⚠ This is the reversal above: the player no longer has to spot a hull
- **A wave is not a broadside.** Its boats arrive slowly, spread over time — the user, 2026-09-02:
  *"Rather than several at once, I think they will come slowly, spread out over time."*
  (「여러 척이 한 번에 온다기보다는 천천히 시간을 두고 올 거 같아」)

## ⚠⚠ Measured 2026-09-02 — what stands today

- **`BOAT_FIRST_SEC` 5.0 · `BOAT_INTERVAL_SEC` 30.0**, and the interval is deliberately one number
  rather than a band (「일정하게」)
- **`BOAT_SPEED_TILES` 1.2 exists because there was no alarm** — 22 조각 makes an 18.3 s crossing so a
  panning player could still find the boat. **With an alarm that reason is spent**
- **The HUD draws no clock at all.** The one clock that existed — 20 s auto-recruit — was deleted
- **A boat is not a wave.** Boats launch one at a time forever; nothing in `battle` groups them

## ⚠ What is NOT decided — **the user answers these before anything is built**

**How long the warning is.** One minute and five minutes were both said, in different sentences —
the shorter one was for the opening wolves and the longer for a raid while the player is off-island.
**Whether the warning time is fixed or grows with the wave.**
**How many boats one wave is, and how far apart they land** — the shape is decided (slowly, spread
out), the numbers are not. `BOAT_INTERVAL_SEC` 30.0 is what stands today and nothing groups boats.
**What the alarm looks and sounds like.** ⚠⚠ **Made, never typed** — it is a thing the player looks
at, so it is built in a tool and loaded.
**Whether the clock stays on screen** after the alarm, or the alarm is a moment and nothing else.
