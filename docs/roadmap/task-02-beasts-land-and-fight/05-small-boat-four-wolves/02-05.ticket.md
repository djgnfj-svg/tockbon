## ⚠⚠ 성채 체력이 같이 내려갔다 — 이 티켓이 부른 결정이다

**`Rules.KEEP_MAX_HP` 240 -> 120** (2026-09-01, the user: 「내려」).

**Why this ticket forced it.** A 늑대 lands 2.0 a second. **At `BOAT_CAPACITY` 8 that is 16 a second and
240 burns in 15 초; at 4 it is 8 a second and 240 burns in exactly 30.0 초** — which is
`BOAT_INTERVAL_SEC` to the digit. **`net_fight` asserts 「a boat ignored whole loses you the run」** and
that went false the moment the hull was swapped.

⚠ **The value was not the builder's to pick and it did not pick it.** It stopped and asked, and the user
chose to lower the health rather than accept 「one boat alone cannot lose you the run」 as a new rule.
**120 restores the 15 초 that 240 was chosen to produce**, so the rule survives the swap unchanged.
