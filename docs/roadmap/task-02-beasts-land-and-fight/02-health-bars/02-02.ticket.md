## Screen

⚠⚠ **`draw_rect` is not a placeholder here, it is what ships** — `CLAUDE.md` carries this failure by

`tools/pixel/` or pixellab and loaded.**

⚠ **The 성채's bar is not a 몸's bar.** A 몸 is a sprite that moves; the 성채 is a 2x2 mesh standing on

## Implementation plan

### What the user answered, 2026-09-01

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Over every 몸, or only picked ones** | **Every 몸** |
| **Does a full bar show** | **No — only a hurt one** |
| **Where the 성채's bar sits** | **Over the roof** |

⇒ **One rule covers all three bars**: over the thing, and only once it has been hit. The 짐승's bar was
already settled as 「위에」 on the same day, so nothing here disagrees with it.

### Seams

**`src/view/` — pooled node state.** A bar is a `Sprite3D` in its own pool beside `_sprites`,
`_outlines` and `_rims`, and a net asserts the fields the engine consumes: position, visible, texture,
`region_rect`, scale.

⚠ **No sim change at all.** `soldier_hp`, `enemy_hp` and `keep_hp` all already exist and already decide
who dies.

### ⚠⚠ The bar is made, and one picture cannot do it

**A bar has to shrink, and a single loaded picture is a fixed width.** ⇒ **two pictures — a frame and a
fill — and the fill is cropped with `region_rect` to the fraction.** Both come out of `tools/pixel/` or
pixellab. **`draw_rect` is what this ticket exists to avoid**, and `CLAUDE.md` carries that failure by
name.

### Files to touch and why

| File | Why |
|---|---|
| `tools/pixel/` or pixellab | **The two pictures.** Frame and fill |
| `src/look.gd` | The bar's width, its lift over a body, its lift over the 성채 roof, the texture paths. ⚠ **Three constants died here 2026-08-28 with the old bar — do not resurrect their values.** Measure new ones |
| `src/view/field_view.gd` | A `_bars` pool and `_paint_bars()`, opened and closed by the same `_hide_unused` the body pool uses |
| `tests/nets/net_fx_view.gd` | The bar's rows |

⚠⚠ **Not `hud_view`.** All three bars stand over something in the world, so all three are `Sprite3D` in
`field_view`. **`hud_view._draw` stays `pass`** and `net_draw_leaf` is not touched.

### Order

1. Make the two pictures.
2. `look.gd` constants, measured off them.
3. `_bars` pool and `_paint_bars` for a 몸.
4. The same pool for the 성채 — a 2x2 mesh on 눈금 2, so the anchor is the footprint's centre plus the
   storey plus the roof.
5. Nets.

### Risk

- ⚠⚠ **Nine 몸 stand on one 블록** (`Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY`). At 「hurt only」 that is at worst nine
  overlapping bars over one 블록. **This ticket does not solve it — it is the first thing to look at on
  screen.**
- **A billboard's scale is thrown away by Godot when it rebuilds the model matrix.** This repo already
  measured that, which is why `_billboard_scale` exists. The bar goes through it or it will not hold.
- **`keep_hp` is 0 on a board with no house.** A bar reading 0/240 there would draw a permanently empty
  bar. **Gate on `keep_tiles.is_empty()`** — the same floor `lost` already uses.

### Acceptance

- A 몸 at full HP has **no visible bar node**; one blow makes one appear.
- The 성채 at 240 has no bar; one blow makes one appear over the roof.
- The fill's `region_rect` width tracks `hp / max_hp` at three different fractions.
- **Nothing new is drawn in `hud_view`** — `net_draw_leaf`'s counts are unchanged.

### Out of scope

- A number, a name, or anything at the screen edge.
- A different drawing for the 짐승's bar than the 검사's — one picture serves all three this round.
- What happens when nine bars overlap.

## Answer

**모든 몸 위에 뜨고, 깎인 것만 뜨고, 성채의 것은 지붕 위다** (2026-09-01, the user, choosing all three
recommendations: *"do all sixteen of those as recommended"* 「이 위에 열여섯 개 물어봤던 거 다 추천대로
좀 해줘」).

**The reasons put with them**: which 무리 is falling apart is the judgement this week asks for, so
picking first is already too late; the round opens with every 몸 and the 성채 full, so a bar that always
shows fills the screen from the first frame; and the 짐승's bar was already over the body, so one rule
reads better than two.
