### Seams

**`src/sim/` only.** A `Battle` built with `.new()` and stepped. ⚠ **Nothing is drawn**, so no view seam
is involved at all.

### What already stands, read in the code

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Appending one 검사 to the roster** | `Army.recruit(slot)` — already appends a row and returns its id |
| **Standing a body beside the 성채** | `Battle.stand_at_keep` into `place_ashore` |
| **A clock that already runs every sub-step** | `_phase_muster`, which counts the revive down |
| **The shape for appending to every column at once** | `land_beast` — it appends to all eight enemy columns and reserves the 조각 in one function |

⚠⚠ **`Battle.setup` sizes the eight soldier columns off `army.type_id.size()`.** A mid-round recruit
therefore has to append to **`Army` and to all eight of them**, or the new row exists on the roster and
nowhere in the fight.

### Files to touch and why

| File | Why |

### Order

1. The two constants.
2. `Battle.recruit()`.
3. The clock in `_phase_muster`.
4. The ceiling.
5. Nets.

### Risk

- ⚠⚠ **The 성채 블록 holds eight, not nine.** A 블록 admits `Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY` = 9 and the house
  takes one 조각 whole. **`stand_at_keep` goes through `place_ashore`, which searches outward** — so the
  ninth does not fail, it stands further out. **Confirm that rather than assuming it.**
  ⚠ **`stand_at_keep` returns -1 when there is nowhere at all, and `_phase_muster` already reads -1 as
  「stay dead」.** **A refused recruit must not consume the clock**, or the ceiling silently becomes
  「however many fit」.
- ⚠⚠ **Appending to `Army` mid-round changes `army.alive.size()`, and `_phase_muster` iterates it.**
  Appending inside that loop is the shape that skips a row or visits one twice.
- **A recruit and a revive both land at `muster_tile`** and compete for the same 조각.
  `REVIVE_SEC` is 20 and it is already running.
- **The starting four are a placeholder the constant itself admits to.** Recruiting on top of a
  placeholder does not make the placeholder measured.
- ⚠⚠ **`MUSTER_PERIOD_SEC` is 20.0 and `REVIVE_SEC` is also 20.0, and they are two rules that happen to
  agree today.** **Do not point one at the other.** The day either moves, folding them would move both
  in silence.
- ⚠⚠ **`MUSTER_CAP` is 9 and `BLOCK_CAPACITY` is also 9, and these are different rules.**
  `BLOCK_CAPACITY` is how many stand on **one 블록**; `MUSTER_CAP` is how many 검사 exist **at all**.
  **Do not point one at the other either** — at the ceiling every 검사 could stand on one 블록, and
  that coincidence is not a rule.

### Acceptance

**All of it headless, with nothing on screen:**

- Step a `Battle` past `MUSTER_PERIOD_SEC` and **one more 검사 stands, alive, on a 조각 next to the
  성채**.
- At the ceiling no more appear, **and the clock does not bank them silently for later.**
- After a recruit, `soldier_state`, `soldier_hp`, `soldier_target`, `soldier_cool`, `soldier_order`,
  `soldier_revive` and the rest are **all the same length as `army.type_id`**.

### Out of scope

- ⚠⚠ **Any screen at all.** No button, no counter, no `draw_rect`. **The user deferred the UI by name**,
  and the day it is built it is made in a tool.
- **A second 성채.** The island has one, so a second is not a thing that can be measured.
- **A resource cost.** 자원 is task 05.
