## What this ticket builds

**Every body carries a hunger value that wears down slowly. At zero its 체력 starts draining, and it
dies if nothing feeds it. A hungry body walks to the 창고 and eats on its own when food is there.**

### Where it came from

> The user, 2026-09-02: ***"There has to be a thing called hunger — a hunger value that slowly wears
> down, and when it hits zero, then 체력 starts being cut."***
> (「허기라는 존재가 있어야 될 거 같은데 허기라는 값이 있어가지고 그게 이제 천천히 닳아서 ... 영이 되면
> 이제 체력이 깎이는 거지」)
>
> ***"They eat on their own — when hungry, build a food store and they go and eat by themselves."***
> (「알아서 먹지. 배고프면은 식량 창고를 만들어놔서 알아서 가서 먹는 걸로 하자」)

## ✅ What is decided

- **허기 is a number per body** that drains with time
- **At zero, 체력 drains; at zero 체력, 죽음** (「체력이 줄다가 죽고」)
- **Nobody feeds.** A hungry body goes to the 창고 (05-08) and eats if food is there
- **허기 shows on the panel** as a fourth line under 이름 · 특성 · 체력 (03-02)
- **Food is by kind**, fish first (05-09); the body eats whatever kind is there

## ⚠ Not decided — numbers, on purpose

**How fast 허기 drains and how fast 체력 drains after** — the user: 「몇 초까지는 너무 커」. **First
values are the builder's and are changed on screen.** ⚠ **Whether an eating body stops what it was doing
(gathering, fighting)** — not asked.

## ⚠⚠ Measured 2026-09-02 — where this lands in the sim

- **`Army` holds nothing per body but `type_id` · `alive` · `slot_id`** — 허기 is the second per-body
  field after 이름 (03-02)
- **체력 draining outside combat is new** — today 체력 moves only when a body is hit, and 피격 plays on
  every drop. **A starving body must not flinch every tick**
- **Walking to eat is an order the body gives itself.** Today every order comes from the hand
