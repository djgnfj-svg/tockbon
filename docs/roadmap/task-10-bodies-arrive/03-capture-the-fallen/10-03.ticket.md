## ⚠⚠ Measured 2026-09-02 — what the sim knows of a fallen body

- **`alive` is a boolean.** There is no 「down」 between alive and dead; 체력 hits zero and `alive` flips
- **The five actions are 대기 · 이동 · 공격 · 피격 · 죽음 and there is no sixth** — a knocked-down body
  has no drawing and no state
- **A 부대 walks to a 칸, not to a body.** 「fetch」 needs an order that targets a thing, which `Hand.order`
  does not have
