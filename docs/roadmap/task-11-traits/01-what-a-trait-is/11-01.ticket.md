## ✅ What is decided

- **Every body has its own** — 특성 is per body, not per 부대
- **Decided at the start of a run** (2026-09-01)
- **Not a rank.** 등급 was cut twice by the user; a trait says what a body is good at, not where it stands
- **요리사 is one** — anyone cooks, a 요리사 cooks differently (05-04 asks how)
- **The body's base numbers are born with it; equipment adds** (log, second round, section 6) — so a
  trait is not the numbers, it sits beside them
- **A rare or flawed body can arrive** (10-01) — which reads as traits that are good and traits that are bad

## ⚠⚠ Measured 2026-09-02 — where a trait would live

- **`Army` holds `type_id` · `alive` · `slot_id` and nothing per body** — 체력 · 공격 · 주기 · 속도 ·
  사거리 all come from the `UNITS` row by species. **Four 검사 are the same body**
- **Nothing on screen names a body.** The HUD draws nothing (03-02), so a trait has nowhere to be read
- **`SWORDSMAN` is the only species and the weapon decides the unit** (03-06, log section 5) — a trait
  is a third axis beside species and weapon, and no code has a third axis

## ⚠ What is NOT decided — **the user answers these before anything is built**

**What a trait changes.** Speed at a job, output of a job, a number the body is born with, or something
a body can do that others cannot.
**How many traits exist**, and **how many one body carries** — one, or a few.
**Whether traits are good only, or good and bad** — 「flawed but usable」 says bad ones exist.
**Whether a trait can change during a run** — 「decided at the start of a run」 says no; 「each body grows
in its own direction」 (2026-09-01) says something grows. **Which grows — the trait, or the numbers?**
**Where it is read.** On the panel 03-02 shows, and 03-02 has not decided what it shows.
