### ⚠ A chase can never catch a walking 늑대, and that is measured

**3.2 against 4.0.** The 검사 loses **0.8 조각 every second** to a 늑대 that is moving. **A chase closes
only on a 늑대 that has stopped**, and a leash long enough to catch a walking one does not exist.

⇒ **The leash is not a balance dial.** It says **how far the player's placement may be disturbed**, and
the order now aims at a 칸, so 2.0 is 「he may step off his post but not off his 칸」.

### ⚠⚠ 「Stop, fight, then carry on」 is the hard half, and it is NOT the chase

**`_phase_movement` opens with an order guard** — an ordered body is skipped before any of this is
reached, and **the chase must sit strictly below that line.** Above it, or running for an ordered body,
is the **double-walk the phase contract forbids**: *「Orders before movement, and movement skips whoever
orders moved」 — falling through walks one body twice in a sub-step, at double speed and toward two
different places.*

⇒ **The leash gives a STANDING 부대 a chase and nothing else.** The user's second sentence is a **third
state** — order held, walk suspended, walk resumed — and **the plan never costed it out**, because the
plan was written while this was still an open question and it lists three shapes without choosing one.

**Why the existing column cannot carry it**: `soldier_order` is **cleared on arrival** inside
`_phase_orders`, which is the exact amnesia the new anchor column is being added to avoid. Suspending an
order means `_phase_orders` skips a body **without clearing it** while `_phase_movement` stops skipping
it — **two guards moving in opposite directions in one sub-step**, and the phase contract is what has to
survive that.
