### Defect 1 — a split 부대 seats a body on ground it can never walk to

**`Hand._seats` hands out a seat on `can_reach`, and `can_reach` is a UNION flood over every picked body.**
`_build_reach` seeds its queue with every 조각 a picked body stands on and floods 8-way through
`Grid.can_step`, so **one member standing on a separate walking component lights that whole component for
the entire 부대.**

**What was measured, driving `Grid`/`Battle`/`Hand` headless with `.new()`:**

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **The board** | **16x8, two islands in one sea** — land at x 1-4 and x 11-14, y 1-6 |
| **Each island** | **24 조각** |
| **`reach` after `pick_many` of four** | **48 조각 — BOTH islands** |
| **`reach_blocks`** | **24 칸** |
| **The 부대** | **four bodies, two on each island** |
| **Pressing a west 칸** | **`order` reports sent = 4** |
| **이동선 for the misseated body** | **none — an empty list** |

⇒ **One east body is given a west seat, and there is no walk from where it stands to that seat.**

**Why `sent` counts it**: `Battle.order_walk` refuses an off-board or impassable 조각 and a body that is
not `ASHORE`, and **it asks nothing about the 조각 the body is standing on** — the west seat is real land,
so it writes `soldier_order` and answers true. Its own header says a reachability test there
would be a second copy of the walking rule. **What then happens is silent**: `_phase_orders` walks the
body, `_walk` finds no better neighbour, the body does not move, and the stall clause at the bottom of
that loop clears the order on the same sub-step. **The player sees a body that simply did not go.**

⚠ **It falsifies a sentence written into `_build_reach`'s own header** — *"A 조각 one member can walk to
lights even if another cannot; the order then seats that member elsewhere, which `_seats` already does."*
**`_seats` does not do that.** It gates every seat on `can_reach`, which is the union, so it cannot tell
which member the 조각 was lit for.

⚠ **It falsifies a second header the same way** — `routes` says *"An empty list for a body means 「it is
already there」, not 「it cannot get there」; `order` refuses an unreachable 칸 before this is ever asked."*
**The empty line in the table above is the second meaning**, because `can_reach_block` is the same union.

⚠ **It cannot be reached in play yet, and here is the shape that would.**

**Today's board splits in two and the 부대 always stands on the big half.** `assets/terrain/island.json`
is 30x26 with **284 land 조각**, of which **280 are one 8-way walking component** and **four stand apart
under the 철광석**: 조각 (20,22) (21,22) (20,23) (21,23), with the ore prop written at 조각 (20,22) offset
0.5, 0.5 — the middle of the four.

⚠⚠ **The 철광석's patch is the shape that would produce this the day anything stands on it** — a second
component with a 검사 on it is exactly the 16x8 board above.

⚠ **The patch is exactly one 칸 and `GLOSSARY.md` is right about it.** `Grid.block_of` sends all four 조각
to block **175** — block column 10, block row 11 — so the day a 검사 stands there it lights that one 칸
whole, and the press that follows is the 16x8 board's press.

⚠ **The four detached 조각 are NOT the four 계단 조각**, which are (6,6) (7,6) (6,7) (7,7) — the same four
02-01 measured. **Both counts are 4 and they are different 조각**, and `.plan-block-move.json`'s

**Bug, not a consequence.** ⚠ **What would settle it**: the user saying what a press should do when the
aimed 칸 sits in a component some of the 부대 cannot enter — send whoever can and report the smaller
`sent`, or refuse the press for everybody.

✅ **CLOSED BY 03-12 ON 2026-09-02 — refuse for everybody.** The 부대's reach became the **intersection**
of every picked body's flood (the user: 「이동이 하나로 떠야지 하나처럼」 — *"the move must show as ONE, as
if they are one"*), so a 칸 only one member can walk to is **not lit** and `order` sends nobody. The 16x8

### Defect 2 — pressing a FULL upper 칸 seats the whole 부대 downstairs

**`Hand._walk_holds` lets a 계단 ride the spill queue, and the 계단 is the only door between storeys.**
When the aimed 칸 has no room, `_seats` spills outward; the spill crosses the 계단, comes down, and every
level-0 조각 is lit and empty.

**What was measured, headless:**

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **The board** | **12x6** — columns 0-5 at level 0, columns 6-11 at level 2 |
| **The 계단** | **one 조각, at (6, 2)** |
| **What seals the top** | **81 짐승** — nine upper 칸 x `Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY` 9 |
| **The 부대** | **nine** |
| **Seats returned for an upper 칸** | **nine, ALL at level 0** |
| **Level gap from the seat to the aimed 칸** | **2** |
| **`Rules.MAX_CLIMB_LEVELS`** | **1** |

⇒ **`Grid.can_strike` refuses a gap of 2, so not one of the nine can hit anything standing on the 칸 the
player pointed at.** The order goes out, nothing goes red, and the 부대 walks to a storey nobody aimed at.

⚠⚠ **This is the same class as the fault fixed on 2026-09-01 and that fix does not catch it.** That day
the spill was gated on `Grid.can_step`, because a 조각 over a cliff edge is an 8-way neighbour by
arithmetic. **A 계단 is a LEGAL `can_step` neighbour**, so the gate passes it — `_walk_holds` then admits
it deliberately (`passable`, and `Grid.is_stair_level` on an odd level) for the reason its own header
gives: without it the spill would be sealed inside one storey.

**Bug, not a consequence** — the 부대 ends up where it cannot fight the thing it was pointed at.
⚠ **What would settle it**: the user saying whether a spill may change storey at all. **Both answers cost
something** — refusing it means a press on a full upper 칸 seats nobody and `sent` comes back 0; allowing
it is what happens today.
