### The places it could live — **none of these is accused**

- **`world_to_screen_px` / `screen_to_world_px`** — the pair carries the yaw through `_ground_right()`
  and `_ground_down()`. **Both look turned.** ⚠ A half-tile origin error is invisible at 0° and doubles

⚠⚠ **The last one is the cheapest to rule in or out and it is the one this ticket should test first**:

### ⚠⚠ The cause — **a half-조각 origin error in `Game._point_at`, invisible at 0°**

`Game._point_at` returns `screen_to_terrain_px(at) / Look.TILE_PX`, and in that unit a 조각's centre is
`(tx + 0.5, ty + 0.5)`. **`soldier_pos` is in the OTHER convention** — `Battle._point_of_tile` is the
integer pair, and `Look.tile_point_px`'s header says the integer IS the 조각 centre. `Hand.body_at`
compares the two against `PICK_BODY_TILES = 0.80`.

⇒ **A press dead on a body's foot already measures 0.71 조각 away, leaving 0.09 of slack.** A press on
the chest or head lands the ground ray 0.44-0.80 조각 further UP THE SCREEN, and screen-up is a world
direction that turns with the yaw: at 0° it is world -y and cancels the +0.5 bias (0.50, picked); at 90°
it is world +x and adds to it (1.07, nothing picked); at 180° it adds on +y; at 270° it cancels on -x.
**So the bias is a defect at every yaw, and a MISS only at 90 and 180** — 「살짝」 and 「각도 꺾었을 때」
both. Subtracting `(0.5, 0.5)` in the probe made it **12/12 at every yaw.**

⚠ **This is the inference step, said out loud**: the user's words fit these numbers, and the user has not
yet confirmed that this is what they saw. Their press on the turned board is the confirmation.
