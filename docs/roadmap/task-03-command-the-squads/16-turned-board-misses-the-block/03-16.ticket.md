### ⚠⚠ What a first measurement should be

**The round trip, per 조각, at each of the four notches.** `FieldView.tile_to_screen_px(tx, ty)` is the
forward projection and `screen_to_terrain_px` → `world_to_tile` is its inverse; **the two must name the
same 조각.** Sweep every land 조각 that is on the glass, at yaw 0 · 90 · 180 · 270, and count how many
come back as a different 조각 and by how much.

⚠ **A probe that does exactly this was written and thrown away on 2026-09-02 without producing a
number** — it died before its first line of output because `Game`'s children are built in `_ready`, and
a `SceneTree` script that reads `game.field_view` on the frame it adds the node reads `null`. **Pump a
frame first.** That is the whole of what the failed attempt is worth.

### The places it could live — **none of these is accused**

- **`world_to_screen_px` / `screen_to_world_px`** — the pair carries the yaw through `_ground_right()`
  and `_ground_down()`. **Both look turned.** ⚠ A half-tile origin error is invisible at 0° and doubles
  along the diagonal once the axes rotate
- **`screen_to_terrain_px`'s stepped ray** — it walks the height ceiling down in `TERRAIN_PICK_STEPS`
  and answers at the first 조각 whose ground reaches that height. **A step coarse enough to skip a ledge
  moves the answer by a 조각, and which ledge is in the way changes with the turn**
- **`Grid.block_of`** — 조각 → 칸. Turn-blind by construction, so an error here would show at 0° too
- **The sweep** — `_sweep_the_yaw` writes the yaw every frame for 0.22 s. **A press DURING the sweep is
  aimed at an angle that is no longer current by the time it is read**

⚠⚠ **The last one is the cheapest to rule in or out and it is the one this ticket should test first**:
press mid-sweep versus press after it settles.
