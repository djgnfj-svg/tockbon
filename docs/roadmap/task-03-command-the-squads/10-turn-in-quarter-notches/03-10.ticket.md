### ⚠⚠ Amended 2026-09-02 after `adversary` — the sweep is stored as REMAINING, never as a target

**A stored target yaw does not work, and the failure is not subtle.**

**⚠⚠ The fourth press never lands.** `turn_by` wraps with `fmod(yaw + deg, 360.0)` and a target does not
wrap with it. From yaw 270 an E press targets **360**; the sweep walks up, the step that would reach 360
comes back through `fmod` as **0**, the remainder is now 360, and **the board spins at 409°/s forever.**
Simulated exactly: 0, 90 and 180 settle in **14 frames**; **270 is still turning at frame 2000.** ⚠
Wrapping the target instead is not the fix either — from 270 with a target of 0 the remainder is **−270**
and the board sweeps three quarters the wrong way. ⚠⚠ **And a net that pumps 「until settled」 does not go
red, it HANGS** — and 티켓 03-15 has already measured what this runner does with a net that never prints:
it takes the count out of 통과 and adds **0** to 실패.

⇒ **The field is `_yaw_remaining`, a signed number of degrees still owed.** Q adds **−90**, E adds
**+90**. Each frame the step is the remainder clamped to the frame's share, `turn_by(step)` is called,
and the step is subtracted. **Nothing is ever compared against an absolute angle, so nothing crosses
360.** Two presses inside one sweep add up; Q then E cancels; the last step is the exact remainder, so
the yaw lands on the notch rather than near it.
