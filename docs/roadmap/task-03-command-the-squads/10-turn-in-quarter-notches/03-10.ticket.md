## What this ticket builds

**Q and E come back and turn the 판 in 90° steps, and the right button's yaw drag is deleted in the
same edit.** After it, the board stands at exactly one of **four** yaws — **0, 90, 180, 270** — and the
right button never moves the camera again, which is what frees it to carry the move order in 03-11.

⚠⚠ **TWO REVERSALS IN ONE TICKET AND BOTH ARE THE USER'S.** Neither old line is erased; the new one
goes on top of both.

⚠ **The user named Don't Starve for the 90° feel.**

### What the camera actually reads, measured in code

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **Opening yaw** | `Look.CAM_YAW_DEG` **0.0** |
| **The new notch** | a NEW `Look.CAM_YAW_SNAP_DEG` **90.0** |
| **The old notch, and it STAYS** | `Look.CAM_YAW_STEP_DEG` **15.0** — the shot tool turns the camera by it |
| **The drag's rate being deleted** | `Look.CAM_YAW_PER_PX_DEG` **0.18** — a 60 px drag turns **10.8°**, a 1280 px one **230.4°** |
| **Everything that reads the yaw** | **three call sites** — `_ground_right`, `_ground_down`, `_place_camera` |
| **Everything that WRITES it in `src/`** | **two** — `turn_by`, which does `fmod(yaw + deg, 360.0)`, and `setup()`, which resets it to `Look.CAM_YAW_DEG` when an island opens |
| **Shader uniforms carrying a yaw** | **zero across all four** — water, pads, prop card, body outline |

⇒ **Four exact values are a CONSEQUENCE of the deletion, not a second rule.** ±90 is exact in float, so
once the drag is gone nothing can put the yaw anywhere but {0, 90, 180, 270} and nothing drifts.

⚠ **Anything that TURNS calls `turn_by`, never writes the yaw field.** `turn_by` holds the ground point
at the middle of the screen and re-clamps; writing the field raw skips both, and that raw write is the
exact mutation `net_camera`'s turn row already exists to catch. ⚠ **`setup()`'s write is not a turn** —
it is the opening reset, and the row that already asserts it (`setup` returns the yaw to `CAM_YAW_DEG`)
must stay green after this ticket.

### ⚠ The one thing the right drag was quietly holding

Its motion branch ends in an early `return`. Deleting the branch also un-skips the **hover plate** and
the **이동선**, which both sat below it and **froze for the whole length of a turn**. That is a fix this
ticket gets for free, and it is worth looking at rather than assuming.

## Screen

**No new asset is made.** The picture is the authored Blender island seen from a fourth of a turn
further round — the mesh, the trees, the 성채 and the 계단 are all already there.

⚠⚠ **What IS new on screen is every body changing which way it is drawn.** `_facing_index` picks a
검사's or a 늑대's sprite by dotting **that body's own heading** against the screen's right and down
axes, and it swaps when the two dots cross — **45° between the body's heading and screen-right.**
**Both rows in `BEAST_TEX` carry four pictures**, so a 90° notch moves every body across **exactly one**
boundary. ⚠ **Not all at the same instant.** The boundary is at 45° in the body's own heading, not at a
yaw of 45°, so a board full of bodies walking different ways flips them at different moments.

## Done when

**One press of E turns the board a quarter and the island is looked at from the new side.** These are
the rows and the shot that say so.

### The four values

- After any number of Q and E presses, `absf(fmod(cam_yaw_deg, 90.0))` is **< 0.001** — never 15, never 10.8
- **Four E presses return the yaw to 0.0**, and the existing row that wraps 300 + 60 to 0 stays green
- **A held Q does not spin the board**: an `echo` press adds **0°**, the same guard the tilt keys carry

### The turn holds the island still

- Across a 90° step the **ground point at the middle of the screen moves < 0.01 px**, the zoom does not
  change, and the real camera's look target does not move — the drift-count row that already asserts
  this at accumulated yaws **15·45·90·165·300**
- ⚠ **That row's own steps are 15·30·45·75·135 and none of them is 90.** It reaches yaw 90 but has
  never taken a 90° step, so **a 90 has to be added to its list** — the plan calling it "the row that
  already proves an instant 90° holds the centre" is reading the label, not the loop

### The right drag is gone, and gone means measurably gone

- A **60 px right drag turns the board 0.00°** — it turned **10.8°** before
- `_turning` has **zero writes left** in the shell; today the right arm is its only one
- On a motion with the right button held, the **hover plate and the 이동선 both still update** — they
  did not, because of the deleted early `return`
- ⚠⚠ **The net function that asserts 「the right button never commands」 certifies nothing today.** Its
  two `ordered == 0` rows sit over an **empty hand** — nothing in that function ever picks a body, and
  the function before it ends on ESC. **They must pick a body first or they stay green after the right
  button starts ordering.**

### ⚠⚠ The one number a screen has to judge — INSTANT or ANIMATED

**Both sides, and the user picks by looking:**

- **INSTANT (0.0 s)** — the sprite flip is **one frame**. Nothing interpolates the yaw today and there
  is no tween anywhere near it, so this is also the smaller change
- **ANIMATED** — Don't Starve, which the user named, does animate it. The plumbing exists: the camera
  is placed every frame from `_process`, so a per-frame `turn_by(step)` needs nothing new. ⚠ **The
  cost is that the sprite crossing becomes visible** — every body flips its picture somewhere inside
  the sweep, each at its own heading's 45°, which reads as the bodies turning rather than the board

⇒ **Done when the user has seen one of the two on screen and said which.** ⚠ **Not done when the model
picks one.**

### ⚠ What the code says and the plan does not

- **`Look.CAM_YAW_PER_PX_DEG` cannot simply be deleted with its shell reader.** The plan calls the
  shell its only reader; **`tools/look/piece_viewer.gd` reads it too, and that reader is live.**
  ⇒ Leave the constant standing with a tombstone, the way the 15° notch already stands for the shot
  tool. **Deleting it breaks the piece viewer.**
- The plan says a grep for a yaw in the view shaders returns nothing. **Three comment lines in the pads
  shader do mention a yaw** — they record a seam measured at yaw 0 and yaw 45. The conclusion still
  holds (**no shader uniform carries a yaw**), but the grep does not.
