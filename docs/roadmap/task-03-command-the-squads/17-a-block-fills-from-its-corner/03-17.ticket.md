### ⚠⚠ Read out of the code, 2026-09-02 — **the user is right and the cause is not subtle**

| Where | What it does |
|---|---|
| **`Hand._seats`** | Seeds its queue with **`Grid.tiles_of_block(block)`** and serves that queue in order |
| **`Grid.tiles_of_block`** | Returns the four 조각 in **raster order** — `for dy: for dx:` ⇒ **north-west, north-east, south-west, south-east** |
| **`Hand._seats`, the inner `while`** | **Fills each 조각 to its own ceiling before moving to the next** |
| **`Rules.TILE_CAPACITY`** | **3** — so the first three bodies all land in the north-west 조각 |
| **`Look.crowd_offset_px`** | Inside ONE 조각: slot 0 at the 조각's centre, the rest on a ring around it |

⇒ **Nothing anywhere asks where the middle of the 칸 is.** Nine bodies fill NW to three, NE to three,
SW to three — **the south-east 조각 stays empty until the tenth body, and there is no tenth**
(`Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY` is 9). ⚠ **The pile is visibly off-centre, toward the north-west, at every
count above one.**

### ⚠⚠ The shape was already CHOSEN and never installed

**2026-08-31, recorded in the roadmap as 「아홉이 서는 모양」**: ***a 3x3 grid across the 칸 that turns to
face the camera.*** The roadmap's own line for it reads **「모양은 정해졌다 — 넣는 일만 남았다」**, and
it has been carrying old ticket number **63**, which has no folder.

⇒ **This ticket is that installation, and 63 is retired into it.** ⚠ **The decision is not re-opened** —
do not re-propose a ring, a hex, or a random scatter.

### ⚠⚠ Where it belongs, and the one thing to get right

**A 3x3 across a 2x2.** The nine seats do NOT line up with the four 조각 one-to-one, so the seat grid is
a VIEW arrangement laid over the 칸 while **`Rules.TILE_CAPACITY` (3 per 조각) and
`Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY` (9 per 칸) stay the sim's ceilings.**

⚠⚠ **The sim owns the slot and the view only reads it** — `_slot_of`'s header in `field_view.gd` says
so, and a seat invented in the view would put two bodies in one place the moment one died. ⇒ **the order
`_seats` hands out has to change, not just the pixels `crowd_offset_px` returns.**
⚠ **「보는 쪽으로 돈다」 means the grid turns with the camera**, so this lands on top of 03-10's yaw and
**must be checked at all four notches.**
