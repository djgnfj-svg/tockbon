### Structure — **the seat lives in `Grid`, beside the slot, and is handed out on arrival**

**Where.** `Grid.hold(unit_id, tile)` and `Grid.release_all(unit_id)` are the only two doors every body
walks through — `place_ashore` (soldiers), the 짐승 landing, `_settle`, the death phases. **`Grid` grows a
per-칸 seat table** beside `reserved`: `block_seats: Dictionary` (칸 → nine unit ids or -1, row-major,
4 = centre). **`hold` gives a body a seat in the 칸 of the 조각 it now holds** if it has none there;
**`release_all` frees it.** A body stepping between two 조각 of the same 칸 keeps its seat; crossing into
another 칸 gets a new one there. **`Grid.seat_of(block, unit_id)` reads it. Nothing else writes it.**

**Which seat.** Centre outward, as a list: **4, then the edge middles 1·3·5·7, then the corners 0·2·6·8.**
Within a tier, the seat **in the body's own 조각 quadrant first** (a corner belongs to its 조각; an edge
middle to either of its two; the centre to all four) — so a 3·3·3·0 walk still seats nine, the ninth
simply stands off its quadrant. **The 성채's 칸**: `fill(KEEP_UID)` takes no seat, its 조각 is simply one
nobody stands in, and the eight bodies take eight seats of the nine. **A 칸 hanging off the board** keeps
the same table; seats over water are never reached because no 조각 there is held.

**Standing point — one sim function, read by the draw AND the pick.** `Battle.stand_point(soldier) ->
Vector2` in `soldier_pos` units: **at rest** (`soldier_order == -1` and the position is a 조각 centre) it is
**칸 middle + `Look.seat_point_tiles(seat, face)`**; **otherwise it is `soldier_pos`**. `enemy_stand_point`
is the same for 짐승. **`Hand.body_at` measures against `stand_point`**, so a body is picked where it is
drawn — `PICK_BODY_TILES` is unchanged and its meaning is restored. ⚠ **`Look.seat_point_tiles` is a static
pure function and `sim` may call it** — it is geometry, not presentation, and lives in `Look` only because
`SEAT_PITCH_TILES` is a presentation constant. If that reads wrong to the user it moves to `Rules`.

**Drawing.** `_put_walker` places the sprite at `stand_point` (the shadow disc likewise). **From walking
to resting the body slides, not pops**: at rest only, the pooled `Sprite3D.position` moves toward the
target at `Look.SEAT_SLIDE_TILES_PER_S` (one constant, ~3 조각/s so 0.7 조각 takes a quarter second);
walking bodies track `soldier_pos` exactly as today. ⚠ **This is the only view-side state, and it is the
sprite's own position — no new field.** The 이동선's first point (`field_view.gd` ~3772) and the falling
bodies (~2019, which pass slot 0 today) both take `stand_point` too. `Look.crowd_offset_px`,
`CROWD_SPREAD_RATIO` and `_crowd_slot_of` are deleted; `PITCH` becomes `Look.SEAT_PITCH_TILES :=
Rules.BLOCK_TILES / 3.0`.

**`Hand._seats` changes one loop, not its type.** It still returns 조각 (`PackedInt32Array`, so
`_route_seats` and the cache compare are untouched) but **round-robins the 칸's 조각 that have room
instead of draining one to three** — so three bodies land in three quadrants and take centre + two edges,
not NW's three seats. `order`, `routes`, `route_points` are unchanged.

**Facing.** `Look.seat_point_tiles(seat, face)` takes it as an argument. **Which value goes in is the open
question above**; until answered, builder does not start. If 「fixed」: `Vector2(0, 1)` at the two call

### ⚠⚠ v2 was attacked too — five more, and the plan above is read WITH these amendments

1. **A seat is held exactly as long as the body holds a 조각 of that 칸 — and the walk never calls
   `release_all`.** `_commit_step` is `hold(dest)` then `_release_except(cur, dest)`, and `_release_except`
   clears the slot only. ⇒ **The seat free hooks into the lowest write of `reserved`**: after ANY slot
   clear (`release_all` and `_release_except` alike), if the uid holds no 조각 in that 칸, its seat there is
   freed. A body mid-step holding 조각 in two 칸 holds a seat in both for the length of one call and the
   old one goes back in the same call. **Invariant, pinned by a net: seats held in a 칸 = distinct holders
   in it, never more than 9**
2. **The slide is keyed on the body's string key, not the pool slot.** `_sprite()` hands out pool slots by
   draw order and every index shifts after a death, so a sprite-held slide would make a body inherit a
   stranger's in-flight position. ⇒ **one view dictionary `_seat_glide: Dictionary` (key → `Vector2`)**,
   beside `_idle_offset(key)`'s kind, dropped when the key leaves the pool. **v2's 「no new field」 is
   withdrawn.** ⚠ **A body's first frame in the pool draws AT its stand point, no slide** — so bodies
   placed by hand in a net read their seat immediately, and the slide only plays on a walk → rest
   transition the view has watched
3. **A 짐승 has no order column, so its rest test is the position alone**: `enemy_pos` exactly on a 조각
