### ⚠⚠ The shape decides how it is BUILT, and that is why this is one ticket and not two

**A corner bracket is ONE picture rotated four times.** A full frame is not: it has to be cut into
**four corners and four stretching edges**, because a selection rectangle is a different size on every
drag. ⇒ **Picking `frame` buys eight sprites and a stretch rule; picking `plain` or `double` buys one
sprite and three rotations.**

⚠⚠ **The stretch is where this repo has already been bitten**, and `gen.py` carries the
measurement in its own words:

> **"The assembly window — an open grimoire. Stretching it with 9-slice misaligns the corners
> (measured in the original PROMPTS.md)."**

⇒ **A 9-slice over the winner is not a free option.** The bracket families sidestep it entirely — four
corners at fixed size, nothing stretched.

⚠ **The user has NOT picked.** Nothing is installed, nothing is in `assets/`, and `src/` draws no box.
