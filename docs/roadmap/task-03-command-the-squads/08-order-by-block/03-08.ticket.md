## What this ticket builds

**The walk order changes unit: the player aims a 칸, not a 조각** (2026-09-01, the user: 「블록단위로
하자」 — *"let us do it by the block"*). `Hand.order`, `Hand.routes` and `Hand.route_points` take a 칸,
the shell converts the cursor's 조각 with `Grid.block_of` on one line at each of the two order sites,
and the 판 marks are pinned one per 칸 so the board can no longer show a unit finer than the press.

⚠⚠ **ONE DESIGN CHANGE RIDES WITH THE UNIT FLIP, AND THE USER DECIDED IT** (the same day: *"the order
goes out even when it is full, and if the 칸 holds enemies they fight"*). **A full 칸 now lights and
accepts the press**; where those bodies actually stand is a second question, answered by `Hand._seats`,
which seats what fits and pushes the surplus into a neighbouring 칸.

⚠ **`reach` stays 조각 and is deliberately not re-pointed.** The mask the picture is painted from is
조각-strided and the shader collapses four texels into a 칸 itself, so two names carry two units:
`can_reach(tile)` and `can_reach_block(block)`. **Both are bare `int` and mixing them lands on a real,
wrong place with nothing going red** — the naming is the only guard there is.

## Screen

**The 판 marks, and nothing else changed on screen.** They are the `pads` child of
`assets/terrain/island.glb` — **a mesh baked in Blender, already in the tree.**

- ⚠⚠ **NO re-bake and NO new asset.** The shader already moves every vertex by its own `UV2` delta;
  pushing `merge` = 1.0 once at adoption grows each of a 칸's four marks into its own quadrant and the
  four tile into one lump. **It is a displacement, not a stack, so the alpha does not multiply**
- **`pad_merge()` is deleted with `Look.PAD_MERGE_ZOOM` 0.72 and `Look.PAD_APART_ZOOM` 1.45** — the
  merge stopped being a function of the camera, so the marks cannot count differently at two zooms
- ⚠ **The shader's 조각 branch is left standing on purpose** — `on_cell`, the `(1.0 - merge)` term and
  the `reach_at(UV.x)` side of the mix are unreachable at merge 1. **This decision has flipped twice
  already** (칸 on 2026-08-28, 조각 on 2026-08-29, 칸 now), and the 조각 look is one uniform away
- **이동선** — the drawn line is unchanged. `route_points` still hands back 조각 units, corner-anchored,
  and the first point is still the body's own `soldier_pos`. ⚠ **What changed is underneath it**: the
  preview now descends `Battle.field_to`, the same flow field `order_walk` descends, so the line and
  the walk cannot come to disagree

### What moved, in code

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `Hand.order` · `routes` · `route_points` | **Take a 칸.** They gate on `can_reach_block` |
| `Hand._seats` | **Replaced `_spread`.** Its seats MAY REPEAT a 조각 — `_spread` handed out distinct 조각 only, and nine bodies do not fit on four distinct 조각 |
| `Hand._standable` | **Lost its `can_hold` clause.** That one union is what made a full 칸 go dark; 「where may I point」 and 「where may a body stand」 are two questions now |
| `Grid.tiles_of_block` | **New — the inverse `block_of` never had.** The same low-corner decode had been hand-copied three times outside `src/` |
| `game.gd` | Two order sites convert with `Grid.block_of`. ⚠ `_tile_at` keeps its 조각 meaning — the hover still wants the square metre |
| `field_view._adopt_the_pads` | `merge` = 1.0 pushed once beside the seven other constants |
| `Battle.field_to` | **`_field_for` renamed and made public**, because `Hand.routes` is now a reader. Three call sites inside `Battle` and one in `Hand` |
| `tests/nets/net_hand.gd` | **New**, and it drives `Hand` with `.new()` and pumps no frame |

⚠ **`Battle.order_walk` KEEPS ITS 조각.** The per-body destination handed to it is still a 조각 seat —
**the aim moved to the 칸 and the walk stayed one square metre**, and `_seats` is the only thing in the
game that crosses between them.

⚠⚠ **But it was touched, on one line, and the change map said it would not be.** The map's own words
are 「`Battle.order_walk` (battle.gd) is untouched」; the file says otherwise at battle.gd, where
`_field_for(tile)` became `field_to(tile)`. **The code is true and the map is stale.** The rename carries
no behaviour: the same body of the same function is now reachable by name from `Hand.routes`, so **the
이동선 and the walk descend the SAME flow field instead of two that happen to be equal.**

### ⚠⚠ Two things the cache and the spill got wrong before they were right

**Both were found by driving `Hand` headless with `.new()`, and neither had anything red to find it.**

1. **The route cache was keyed on the 칸 alone and it lied.** `_spread` read only `reach`, frozen at
   the pick, so its answer could not move; **`_seats` reads live occupancy** and moves every frame.
   Hover a 칸, let nine bodies fill it, hover again — **the preview still ended on 조각 54 while
   `order` seated the 부대 on 조각 41. Three bodies, three wrong lines.** The key is now the seats and
   the starts.
2. **The spill crossed a cliff.** Two 조각 either side of a tier edge are 8-way neighbours BY NUMBER
   and no body may cross between them. On a 12x6 board with columns 0-5 at level 0 and 6-11 at level 2,
   **twelve bodies ordered onto the upper 칸 seated nine upstairs and dropped three on 조각 5,
   downstairs across the wall.** The walk outward now asks `Grid.can_step` — **the walker's own
   question, not a second one that can drift from it.**

### ⚠⚠ What is NOT closed

- **Seating inside a 칸 does not honour which quarter was pressed.** With the marks merged the press
  carries no sub-칸 information at all, so even a single body is seated by `_seats` rather than dropped
  on a square the player picked out. ⚠ **This is what a 부대 formation would replace**, and the
  roadmap's shape 6 for nine standing bodies is the thing that plugs into that one function
- **Which neighbouring 칸 takes the overflow is BFS discovery order, and nobody chose it.** The walk
  inherits `_spread`'s 8-way `dy`/`dx` table, which was an arbitrary tie-break. ⚠ **The 성채's 칸 seats
  eight and not nine** — `Grid.block_hold_count` counts the house as one body — **so the ninth body's
  destination is on screen every time**
- **No net reads a single `PAD_*` constant.** The seven look numbers were judged by eye against a
  조각-sized quad; at merge 1 the same alpha covers four times the area and the same `PAD_HOVER_LIFT`
  0.06 lifts a 2x2 lump. **That is a judgement at the screen and it belongs to the user**
