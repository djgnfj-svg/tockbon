## Two definitions arrived an hour apart and they do not obviously agree

**By movement**, 2026-09-01: *"The 일반병 should be able to move as a squad and also move as one. The
병사 stay bunched."* (「일반병은 그 부대로도 움직일 수 있고 한 명으로도 움직일 수 있어야 될 거 같은데?
병사들은 뭉쳐있는 건데」)

**By the hand**, the same day: *"There is a thing called 병사, and if you equip a weapon it becomes one
— so what do we call the plain thing with nothing at all? And if you put a farm tool in its hand it
becomes a 농부."*

⚠⚠ **농부 holds a farm tool and still has to go alone.** ⇒ **「moves alone」 and 「holds no weapon」 are
NOT the same set.** The only rule that makes them agree is **「holds a weapon ⇒ moves only as a squad」**,
**and nobody has said that.**

## ⚠⚠ Why this one decides whether gathering costs anything

**일반병 fights but is weak** — 「일반병은 싸운데 약해야지」. ⇒ **sending one out to gather is sending
away a defender, and the choice has a price.**

**If a gathering body cannot fight at all**, gathering and defending never compete, and **that is the
sentence both dead games died on**: 「an advantage with no cost is not a decision」.

✅ **Dissolved on 2026-09-02, fourth round — there is no 「moves only as a squad」 set any more.**

When asked whether a body holding a weapon moves only as a squad, the user answered:

> ***"The 부대 is a bit ambiguous — the concept has gone vague, so it needs thought. In practice moving

> soldier, becomes a bit precious."***
> (「이게 부대라ㅡㄴ ㄴ개념이 애매해져서 곰니해야함 사실상 하나씩 옮기는게 정설이 되ㅕㄴ서 말이지 그래서
> 캐릭하나하나가 병사하나하나가 좀 소중해지는 게임이 될듯함」)
>
> And one round later: ***"The 부대 is a bit ambiguous — for now, manage each character one by one."***
> (「부대가 살짝 애매함 일단 캐릭터 하나하나 관리하는걸로 해야할듯함」)
>
> Asked whether the 병사 / 일반병 split survives when every body moves alone: ***"It does not."***
> (「안 남는다」)

⇒ **The three questions above dissolve together:**

- **「무기를 들면 부대로만 움직이나」** — no. Every body is sent alone; several picked is a convenience
- **「일반병이 부대에 들어가 싸우나」** — there is no 일반병. **The body is ONE kind**; what it holds
  (04-04) and its 특성 (11-01) say what it is good at
- **「얼마나 약한가」** — nothing to weaken. Numbers are born with the body and equipment adds (log,
  2026-09-02 second round, section 6)

⚠⚠ **What this does to gathering's price**: the price was 「sending a defender away」. With one kind of
body that is still true — **every body sent to gather is a body not defending** — so the sentence both
dead games died on is not re-opened. ⚠ **What 부대 means is NOT settled**; 03-05's hotkeys wait on it.
