## Two definitions arrived an hour apart and they do not obviously agree

**By movement**, 2026-09-01: *"The 일반병 should be able to move as a squad and also move as one. The
병사 stay bunched."* (「일반병은 그 부대로도 움직일 수 있고 한 명으로도 움직일 수 있어야 될 거 같은데?
병사들은 뭉쳐있는 건데」)

**By the hand**, the same day: *"There is a thing called 병사, and if you equip a weapon it becomes one
— so what do we call the plain thing with nothing at all? And if you put a farm tool in its hand it
becomes a 농부."*

⚠⚠ **농부 holds a farm tool and still has to go alone.** ⇒ **「moves alone」 and 「holds no weapon」 are
NOT the same set.** The only rule that makes them agree is **「holds a weapon ⇒ moves only as a squad」**,
**and nobody has said that.**

## ⚠⚠ Why this one decides whether gathering costs anything

**일반병 fights but is weak** — 「일반병은 싸운데 약해야지」. ⇒ **sending one out to gather is sending
away a defender, and the choice has a price.**

**If a gathering body cannot fight at all**, gathering and defending never compete, and **that is the
sentence both dead games died on**: 「an advantage with no cost is not a decision」.
