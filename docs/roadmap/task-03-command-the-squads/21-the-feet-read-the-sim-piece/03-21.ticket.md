## What this ticket builds

**A walking body that is never drawn on a 조각 it could not stand on.** The seat offset still pushes the

## ⚠⚠ Measured 2026-09-03 — the defect, in numbers

| What was measured | Result |
|---|---|
| **Hand-drawn island** | **46 walkable level-0 조각** whose drawn point can take a level-2 height |
| **The lift** | **exactly 1.00 조각 — one whole storey.** Not a wobble |
| **A run of them** | **(8,3) through (12,3) are consecutive** — five 조각 of walking along the top of the cliff |
| **Generated islands, 100 seeds** | **100 of 100 carry the defect**, averaging **33.2 조각** each |
| **칸 whose 조각 differ in level** | **0** on the hand island (195 칸) and **0** across all 100 seeds |

⇒ **The island is not the fault and neither is the generator.** Both obey the rule that a 칸 is one level.
**A resting body cannot lift** — `adversary` gave the reason the first draft did not have: `SEAT_PITCH_TILES`
is 2/3, so `Look.seat_point_tiles` reaches at most **0.943 조각** from the 칸's middle, the middle is 0.5 from
a 조각 centre, and every seat therefore rounds back inside its own 칸. **Only a walking body lifts**, because
it carries its seat offset ACROSS a 칸 boundary into a neighbouring 칸 a storey up.

## ⚠⚠ Why it happens — the one step nobody else takes

`Look.SEAT_OFFSET_MAX_TILES` is `Rules.BLOCK_TILES * 0.5` = **1.00 조각**, and a 조각 is one tile wide.
`Grid.surface_h` **rounds** its point to a 조각 — deliberately, so the sim stays reproducible. **Rounding a
point offset by a whole 조각 lands on the neighbouring 조각.**

**How others avoid it** — the material is in `docs/reference/2026-09-03-grid-sim-under-a-3d-mesh.md`:

- **Larian's AI grid** bakes one height per cell and never raycasts the render mesh under a moving body —
  their own docs say that is what *"prevents you from running off a wall of a cliff"*
- **Trudy's Mechanicals** lerps the visual Y *"between the bottom of the cube and its top while walking"* —
  **both endpoints are logical cell heights**, so the drawn body can never take a third value

⇒ **Height is a function of the logical 조각; the visual offset only interpolates between logical heights.**

## ⚠⚠ The plan that was rejected, and why — keep this, do not delete it

**「Read the foot height at the sim's point, leave the drawn point alone.」** It matches the references and it
is one line. `adversary` measured four things against it:

1. **The ground shadow is NOT flat screen geometry.** `_put_ground_shadow` → `_g_disc` → `_g_tri` →
   `_ground_y_px`, which wears the terrain's own height at every vertex — and it picks the SAME 조각 the foot
   does (`tile_point_px` puts the point at `p + 0.5`, `_ground_y_px` floors it, so `floor(drawn + 0.5)` IS
   `round(drawn)`). **Today feet and shadow agree on the cliff top. Dropping only the feet splits them by a
   whole storey**
2. **The 이동선's first point does the same** — `_paint_move_lines` starts at `_drawn_of(...)`, so the route's
   tail would begin on the cliff top and run down the rock face
3. **The dying branch would grow a NEW pop.** Today walking and dying read one point, so there is none;
   dropping the walking feet alone makes a body jump a storey UP for the 0.72 초 of its death
4. **The body would be drawn INSIDE the cliff at all 46 조각, not some of them.** Lifting and burying have
   the same condition — the offset pointing at the higher 조각 — so it is one set, and depth testing hides
   the card behind the rock

⇒ **The user chose the other shape**: 「그려지는 점이 절벽을 안 넘는다」.

## ✅ What is decided

- **The drawn point never lands on a 조각 whose 눈금 differs from the sim's 조각.** One place, and the feet,
  the shadow, the 이동선 and the death animation all inherit it because they all read the drawn point
- **The offset is SHORTENED, not dropped.** It shrinks toward zero as the body approaches a ledge, so there
  is no pop — 9/2's 「on a move order they depart as if newly spawned」 fix is not being undone
- **`Look.SEAT_OFFSET_MAX_TILES` keeps its value.** Shrinking the constant would hide the defect at some
  magnitudes and leave it at others
- **Nothing in `src/sim/` moves.** The climb rule already refuses the cliff and it was never wrong
- **The stair is untouched** — the sim's own position is continuous along a run (`adversary` confirmed
  `Battle._walk` and `_glide` interpolate and never snap), so `surface_h` still returns the sloping tread

## Implementation plan — ⚠⚠ REWRITTEN AFTER ROUND 3. The first plan is kept below it.

**The rule is a LENGTH CAP, not a ray.** `FieldView._offset_kept_on_level(grid, at, off)` caps the
offset's LENGTH by the distance from `at` to the nearest 조각 square whose 눈금 differs — in any
direction. It does not look at `off`'s direction at all. `_ledge_reach(grid, at, limit)` scans rings
outward, stops when it cannot improve, and answers `INF` when there is no ledge within reach, which is
what makes `off` come back bit-for-bit unchanged away from a cliff.

`_glided` runs BOTH branches through it — the walking answer AND the resting glide's straight line —
so the feet, the shadow, the outline, a dying body and the 이동선's first point all inherit it.
`Look.LEDGE_INSET_TILES` is the hair it backs off by.

### Why the ray rule was thrown away — it introduced a pop the game did not have

**The first build walked out along `off` and stopped before the first differing 조각.** It fixed the
cliff-float and it made the drawn point DIRECTION-DEPENDENT: when `at` crossed a 조각 line the base 눈금
flipped, a ledge that had been behind the body was suddenly in front of it, and the answer went from
「no shrink」 to 「shrink to nothing」 in one frame.

| Measured on the ray rule, `at` swept 0.02 조각, only transitions `can_step` allows | |
|---|---|
| **Worst jump in one frame** | **0.960 조각** |
| **Jumping positions, hand island** | **168** |
| **Jumping positions, seeds 1 · 2 · 3** | **168 · 174 · 156** |

⚠⚠ **A walking body's own 눈금 only changes at a stair, so this read as: every time a body walks onto or
off a stair, its picture snaps sideways almost a whole 조각.** Before this ticket the drawn point was
`at + off` and perfectly continuous — **the ray rule would have traded the cliff-float for a stair
teleport.** 「Accept it」 was proposed and refused on those numbers.

### What the length cap measured

| | |
|---|---|
| **Worst jump** | **0.0403 조각** against a bound of 0.08 — twenty-four times smaller |
| **Inversion (the ray rule, same sweep)** | **0.960 조각 over 407 steps** |
| **조각 whose seat is pulled in** (hand island + 10 seeds) | **880 of 4340** |
| **조각 whose seat collapses to nothing** | **0.** The shortest pulled seat is **0.4990 조각** |

⇒ **The feared 「row of dead-centred bodies along the cliff」 does not happen.** A resting body stands on
a 조각 centre and the neighbouring square is half a 조각 away, so the cap can never fall below 0.5 for a
body at rest. Only a body mid-crossing sees zero.

⚠ **The bound 0.08 is DERIVED, not tuned**: the cap is 1-Lipschitz in `at`, so away from a line the
step (0.02) plus the cap's change (0.02) is 0.04, and on a line 0.06 is the arithmetic ceiling.
**Tightening below 0.06 would redden one day for no reason.**

### ⚠⚠ One row was WEAKENED and it is said out loud

「a resting body is not moved a hair by the rule」 became **「a resting body AWAY FROM A LEDGE」**. The ray
rule only shortened offsets pointing at a cliff; the length cap shortens every direction, so a body
resting beside a ledge really is pulled in. **Two new rows were written to measure that price instead of
hiding it** — the pull at 조각 (8,3), and that it never goes under 0.49 조각.

## Implementation plan — the FIRST one, kept because a reader will propose it again

**A ray**: base 눈금 from `round(at)`, solve the 조각-line crossings `at + t*off = k + 0.5` on both axes,
sort them, take the first stretch whose 눈금 differs, back off by an inset. Continuous in `at` along a
straight ledge and **discontinuous where the base flips** — which is the whole reason it was replaced.
Two holes were found in it and both are worth knowing:

- **The stretch from `t = 0` to the first crossing was assumed to be the base 조각 and never sampled.**
  With `at` exactly on a 조각 line and `off` pointing backwards that assumption is false — **770 of
  20448 cases on the hand island.**
- **The resting branch's `move_toward` was not clamped at all** — a straight line from the last walking
  point to the seat, unchecked. **224 of 70880 cases on the hand island crossed a differing 눈금, worst
  at 조각 (8,3): 23% of a 1.90 조각 glide, about 0.15 초, a whole storey up.** ⚠⚠ **That is what the user
  was still seeing after round 1**, and it is why 「the fix looks like it did nothing」 was true.
