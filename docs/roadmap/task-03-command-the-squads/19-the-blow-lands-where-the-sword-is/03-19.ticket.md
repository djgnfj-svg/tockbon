## What this ticket builds
**The picture of a blow and the moment its damage is dealt are the same moment**, for both the 검사 and
the 늑대 — and a body that slides into its seat walks instead of breathing.

### What was observed
> The user, with the game open on `main`: ***"This — the game's animation and the code's action do not
> match."*** (「이거 게임 애니메이션하고 코드적 액션이 안맞음」)
> Then: ***"Can you match those up?"*** (「그것좀 맞춰줄 수 있나?」)

### Read out of the code — three places disagreed, and the first is the one the user saw
| Where | What it did |
|---|---|
| **`Battle._phase_attacks`** | The cooldown reached 0 → **the damage was dealt on that sub-step** and the cooldown wound back to the period. There was no swing in the sim at all |
| **`FieldView._fx_step`** | Saw the cooldown RISE and started the eight-frame attack strip (0.96 s) **from that frame** — so the victim's flinch, its bar, the flash, the shards and the number all fired on frame 1 of the striker's swing, and the lunge reached full extension 0.36 s later |
| **`FieldView._fx_step`, the hp-drop branch** | A hit **zeroed the striker's own attack clock**. With damage already dealt on frame 1, two bodies trading blows (2.4 s vs 2.0 s periods) kept showing **damage without any swing** |
| **`FieldView._fx_step`, the walk branch** | `still` and `head` were measured off the **sim's position**, but a body that has arrived glides to its seat over up to two 조각 at 3 조각/s while the sim's point stands still — so it **breathed while it slid** |

### What was built
- **The sim got a swing.** `Rules.SWING_LAND_SEC` = 0.4 s (24 sub-steps exactly). A ready body with a
  target in reach **starts** a swing: cooldown → period, `*_swing` → 0.4, the target locked in
  `*_swing_at`. The damage is dealt on the sub-step the swing clock runs out, against the locked target,
  and only if it is still alive, standing and in reach — otherwise the swing whiffs and still costs the
  period. `*_blows` counts landed blows and only ever rises.
- **A body hit mid-swing still lands it.** Cancelling on a hit would let the faster period interrupt the
  slower one every time; the 검사 would never land on a 늑대.
- **The view starts the strip on the cooldown rise (unchanged) and fires the flash, shards, number and
  hit-stop on a `*_blows` rise** — the one honest 「the blow landed」 the sim has. A whiff shows nothing.
- **The flinch no longer cancels a swing whose blow has not landed.** `_body_tex` puts the swing on top
  until 0.4 s in, the flinch on top after. The glossary's 「맞으면 때리던 것을 놓는다」 carries the
  exception now.
- **Legs and facing read the drawn point** (the seat glide's entry), not the sim's — so the slide into a
  seat is walked, and the breath starts when the slide ends.
- **The 0.4 s sits on the fourth attack frame (0.36–0.48 s), inside the lunge's hold window
  (0.365–0.528 s).** A net in `net_fight` holds `Rules.SWING_LAND_SEC` inside that window, so moving
  either the strip or the constant reddens.

the landing; a swing in progress is not hidden by a flinch; a sliding body walks.** Branch
`worktree-anim-match`, commit `4f534487`, not on `main`.
