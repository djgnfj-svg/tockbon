## What this ticket builds

**A right press on a lit 칸 sends the picked 부대 there, and the left button stops ordering.** Today one
button does both jobs: `_press_the_island` (`src/shell/game.gd`) asks `hand.is_empty()` and branches
— a full hand walks, an empty hand picks. **This ticket cuts that branch in half along the button.** The
full-hand arm (673-690: `Grid.block_of` → `hand.can_reach_block` → `hand.order` → `_let_go`) becomes the
right button's whole job; the empty-hand arm (692-697: `hand.body_at` → `hand.pick`) stays on the left.

⚠⚠ **The right button has ONE job and therefore NO press-vs-drag split.** `Look.DRAG_PAN_THRESHOLD_PX`
is **6.0** (`src/look.gd`) and it is never consulted for the right button — no travel gate is
written for it. ⚠ **The left button keeps its own split**: a short press picks one body, a drag
box-selects. That half is **03-12** and none of it is built here.

⚠ **Until 03-12 lands, `hand.ids` is length 1**, so "the whole 부대" is a claim about a list of one.
⚠⚠ **A list of one can still be sent NOWHERE.** `_standable` stopped asking capacity on 2026-09-01, so
a full 칸 lights; `order` then stops at `seats.size()` (`src/sim/hand.gd`) and `_seats`' own
header calls fewer seats than bodies **not an error** (481-482). ⚠ **`_let_go()` runs at `game.gd`
BEFORE the count is read at 685**, so a press that seated nobody empties the hand anyway — which is why
the row below asks where the body went and not only whether the hand is empty.

### ⚠⚠ One thing here is NOT decided, and the user is the one who decides it

**What a LEFT press on empty ground does to a full hand is an open question.** The live rule is the
user's own sentence:

> The user, 2026-08-31: ***"unless I press ESC, the move should stay the priority."***
> (「이게 esc를 하지 않는 이상 이동 우선으로 해줘야할듯한데」)

That sentence is written into the shell at `game.gd` as **"A PRESS THAT CANNOT BE A WALK KEEPS THE
HAND, AND ESC IS THE ONLY WAY TO LET GO"**, and the precedence it argues for sits above it at 664-672.

⚠⚠ **This ticket reverses nothing and writes no line for it.** After the split, a left press on empty
ground falls into the empty-hand arm, `hand.body_at` (692) answers -1, and the press returns false —
**the hand survives, and `game.gd`'s ESC still lets go.** ⚠ **A left press on a BODY does change**,
and that half is the split itself rather than a decision: the pick arm re-picks, so a full hand no
longer swallows the press.

⚠ **The reason the old rule existed is spent all the same** — it existed because ONE button did both
jobs, so a press aimed a little wide lost the 부대 the player had just picked. **Three answers are on
the table and the model picks none of them:**

- **A left press on nothing clears the hand** — StarCraft, which the user named; ESC stays as a second
  way;
- **A left press on nothing does nothing** — today's rule, and what this ticket builds by falling
  through;
- **A left press on a body replaces the selection, a press on nothing does nothing.**

⚠⚠ **Whichever the user picks, the old line is written onto and never deleted**, and the rejected
branch goes to `docs/design/`.
