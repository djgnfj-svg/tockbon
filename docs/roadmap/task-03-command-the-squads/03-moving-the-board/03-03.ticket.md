ways of moving the camera deleted, one after another, in one conversation:

### ⚠⚠ What was measured, headless, after the deletions

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **A 300 px drag sideways** | the board moves **393.8 px** |
| **The same drag, started on the 조각 a body is standing on** | **393.8 px — identical**, and **zero walk orders are issued** |
| **A 300 px drag downward** | **584.9 px** — the pitch stretches the vertical axis |

⇒ **The drag works.** ⚠⚠ **What is actually true is the last row**: the whole roam range is NARROWER
than one screen, so travelling feels like nothing moves — **there is barely anywhere to go.** The
6 px threshold (`Look.DRAG_PAN_THRESHOLD_PX`) is what keeps a drag over land from being eaten by the
walk order, and it is holding.

### The question this ticket exists to answer

**What moves the board, now that the user has deleted three of the four ways?** Everything below is a
statement of what stands today, not a proposal:

- **The left button is doing two jobs**: a press in place commands a body, a press that travels 6 px
  looks around. **One button, two meanings, separated by six pixels**
- **The right button turns, the wheel zooms, R and F tilt.** Those three were never questioned
- **The roam bound is `Look.CAM_ROAM_TILES` of sea** around the island, and it is what makes the range
  1120 px. **Nobody has looked at that number on a screen**

## Answer

**Answered by the user on 2026-09-02, and it is the whole control layout.** ⚠⚠ **All three of the
2026-08-31 deletions come back, and the right button's yaw drag goes out in their place. That is ONE
reversal of one day's decision, not three separate ones.**

| Hand | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left, short press** | pick one body (unchanged) |
| **Left, drag** | box-select every 검사 inside the box — **NEW**, and it is what takes the pan away |
| **Right, press** | order the picked 부대 onto that 칸 — **NEW**. ⚠ It does nothing else, so it needs no press-vs-drag threshold |
| **Q and E** | turn the board in 90° steps, four orientations only — **RESURRECTED**, and it turned 15° a notch before |
| **WASD** | pan the board — **RESURRECTED** |
| **Screen edge** | pan the board — **RESURRECTED** |
| **Wheel** | zoom (unchanged) |
| **R and F** | step the tilt (unchanged) |

**How it was settled.** The user asked what RimWorld does — it pans with the keyboard and the screen
edge, its left button selects and drags a box, its right button moves, and **it has no camera rotation
at all**. Bad North, which is this project's bar, turns with A and D, the mouse and the right stick —
and this island is a 3D board pitched at 40° with a second storey, one 계단, a 성채 and thirty-one pine
trees, so **something is always behind something.** ⇒ **the turn stays, the keys pan.**

⚠ **A/D could not take the turn** the way Bad North does, because WASD had just taken the pan. **Q and
E were free**, and the user named Don't Starve for the 90° feel.

> The user, on removing the right button's turn: ***"rotating with the right mouse does not really have
> any advantage."*** (「오른쪽 마우스로 회전을 하면 뭔가 장점이 별로 없어서」)

⚠⚠ **The right-drag yaw was itself the user's own request on 2026-08-26 and was re-taken on
2026-08-30.** **Deleting it reverses a reversal** — the new line goes on top of both and neither is
erased.
