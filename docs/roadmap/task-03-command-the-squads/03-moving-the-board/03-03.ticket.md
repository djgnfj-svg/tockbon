**What is left is the left button's drag, and it is the whole of it.** ⚠ **The user then asked
「맵이 이동이 안되는 문제가 있는거잖아?」 — and the premise is not what the numbers say.**

### ⚠⚠ What was measured, headless, after the deletions

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **A 300 px drag sideways** | the board moves **393.8 px** |
| **The same drag, started on the 조각 a body is standing on** | **393.8 px — identical**, and **zero walk orders are issued** |
| **A 300 px drag downward** | **584.9 px** — the pitch stretches the vertical axis |
| **How far the camera may travel east-west at all** | **1120 px**, against a **1280 px** screen |

⇒ **The drag works.** ⚠⚠ **What is actually true is the last row**: the whole roam range is NARROWER
than one screen, so travelling feels like nothing moves — **there is barely anywhere to go.** The
6 px threshold (`Look.DRAG_PAN_THRESHOLD_PX`) is what keeps a drag over land from being eaten by the
walk order, and it is holding.

### The question this ticket exists to answer

**What moves the board, now that the user has deleted three of the four ways?** Everything below is a
statement of what stands today, not a proposal:

- **The left button is doing two jobs**: a press in place commands a body, a press that travels 6 px
  looks around. **One button, two meanings, separated by six pixels**
- **The right button turns, the wheel zooms, R and F tilt.** Those three were never questioned
- **The roam bound is `Look.CAM_ROAM_TILES` of sea** around the island, and it is what makes the range
  1120 px. **Nobody has looked at that number on a screen**
