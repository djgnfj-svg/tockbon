### ⚠ Why the four-direction art bears on the rotation half

**Every body is four drawings** — 정면우·정면좌·후면우·후면좌 — and `field_view._facing_index` picks
between them. **If the camera turns freely, the angle at which one drawing gives way to the next is a
free variable.** Fixed angles make the camera and the art a one-to-one match. ⚠ **This is a reason, not
a decision** — the user picks.

## Answer

**Answered by the user on 2026-09-02. The gesture is StarCraft's, and the board found another hand.**

**The left button splits by travel, exactly as it does today**: a short press picks one body, a drag
box-selects every 검사 inside the box. **The pan it displaces went to WASD and the screen edge**
(ticket 03-03), so nothing is left without a hand.

⚠⚠ **A LIVE RULE REVERSES WITH IT.** **A left press on empty ground now CLEARS the 부대.** What it
replaces is the user's own sentence, written into the shell as *"A PRESS THAT CANNOT BE A WALK KEEPS
THE HAND, AND ESC IS THE ONLY WAY TO LET GO"*:

> The user, 2026-08-31: ***"unless I press ESC, the move should stay the priority."***
> (「esc를 하지 않는 이상 이동 우선으로 해줘야할듯한데」)

**That rule existed because ONE button did both jobs and a wide press would otherwise lose the 부대.**
**With two buttons the reason is gone**, and StarCraft — which the user named — clears on a click on
empty ground. ⚠ **ESC stays as a second way**, so nothing the player already learned stops working.

⚠⚠ **THE BOX IS A PICTURE AND IT IS NOT PULLED YET.** CLAUDE.md: anything the player looks at is made
in a tool and loaded. **Sixteen candidates were pulled on 2026-09-02 in four families and the user has

with nothing on screen is the one gap this cannot close.
