### ⚠⚠ What the gesture costs, and it is not the gesture

**The left button already carries two jobs.** `03-03` measured them: a press in place commands a body,
a press that travels `Look.DRAG_PAN_THRESHOLD_PX` (6 px) pans the board.

⇒ **A drag that SELECTS is a third job on the same button, and it wants exactly the motion the pan
already owns.** ⚠ **One of the two has to move**, and that is what this ticket decides.

⚠ **The user deleted three of the four ways of moving the camera on 2026-08-31** — the QE keyboard turn,
the pointer parked at the window edge, and WASD. **The left drag is what is left of panning**, so
handing that motion to selection empties the box.

### ⚠ Why the four-direction art bears on the rotation half

**Every body is four drawings** — 정면우·정면좌·후면우·후면좌 — and `field_view._facing_index` picks
between them. **If the camera turns freely, the angle at which one drawing gives way to the next is a
free variable.** Fixed angles make the camera and the art a one-to-one match. ⚠ **This is a reason, not
a decision** — the user picks.

- **What a drag-selection does when it catches bodies standing on two different 블록** — the 블록 is

- **Whether a selection survives a camera move**, and whether it survives a rotation
