### How ONE body is picked today, end to end

| Step | Symbol | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `_begin_press(at)` | The left press, and **the only button that opens a press.** Stores `_press_at`, sets `_press_open`, clears `_panning`. **Nothing is ordered and nothing is panned here** |
| 2 | the motion branch | `motion.position.distance_to(_press_at) > Look.DRAG_PAN_THRESHOLD_PX` (**6.0 px**) latches `_panning`, and every motion after it calls `field_view.pan_by(motion.relative)`. ⚠ **Measured from the press point, never summed from `relative`** |
| 3 | `_end_press()` | `if _press_open and not _panning and battle != null` → `_press_the_island(_press_at)`. **A press that travelled never commands** |
| 4 | `_press_the_island` | Empty hand ⇒ `hand.body_at(battle, _point_at(at), Look.PICK_BODY_TILES)`. `_point_at` is `screen_to_terrain_px(at) / Look.TILE_PX`, **fractions kept on purpose** |
| 5 | `Hand.body_at` | Scans `battle.ashore_ids()`, **nearest wins**, distance taken against `soldier_pos` in 조각 units, radius **`PICK_BODY_TILES` 0.8 조각** |
| 6 | `hand.pick(battle, who)` | Three lines: builds a one-long `PackedInt32Array` and calls `pick_many` |
| 7 | `_tell_the_view()` | `set_reach(hand.reach)` · `set_picked(hand.ids)` · `set_move_lines([])`, **all three together** |

⚠ **The press has slack the box will not have.** `PICK_BODY_TILES` is 0.8 조각 and `look.gd` says out
loud it is generous because a body is drawn standing up from its feet. **A rectangle is exact** — what
it catches is decided to the pixel, so where the body is drawn stops being a rounding question.

### What `Hand.pick_many` already accepts — **the sim half is built**

- **`pick_many(battle, want: PackedInt32Array)` takes a list of ANY length.** It drops ids outside the
  columns, drops ids that are not `ASHORE`, dedupes, and calls `_build_reach` **once**. It answers
  false only when nothing at all survived the filter.
- **`pick` is a wrapper over it.** The file's own header: ***"there is no 「one body」 case — there is a
  list whose length happens to be 1."***
- **It is already driven with twelve at once**: `net_hand.gd` picks a 부대 of twelve on the cliff
  fixture with `pick_many` and asserts the whole board lights bar one 계단.
- **The candidate set is player-only by construction.** `Battle.ashore_ids()` walks `soldier_state`
  and nothing else, so ***"a box picks 검사 only"*** is not a filter anybody has to write.
- **The roster ceiling is `Rules.SWORDSMAN_START_COUNT` 4 growing to `Rules.MUSTER_CAP` 9**, one body
  per `Rules.MUSTER_PERIOD_SEC` 20 s. ⚠⚠ **That nine is NOT `Rules.BLOCK_CAPACITY`'s nine** — one is
  how many 검사 exist, the other is how many bodies stand on one 칸, and `rules.gd` already warns not
  to write either as the other.

⇒ **What is missing is the shell's hit test and the picture. Not the hand.**

### What turns a body's world position into a screen point

**`FieldView.world_to_screen_px(world, ground_h)`, and it is PURE.** It reads four camera fields —
`cam_px`, `zoom`, `cam_yaw_deg`, `cam_pitch_deg` — through `_visible_ground_px`, `_ground_centre_px`,
`_ground_right` and `_ground_down`. **No `battle`, no `get_node`, no tree.** That is the「pure camera
functions」surface `GLOSSARY.md` names, and `net_camera` already drives it with `.new()`.

⚠⚠ **`tile_to_screen_px` is NOT pure** — it goes through `_ground_h`, which reads `battle.grid.level`.
**Use the former.**

⚠ **The height argument is `_stand_h(p)`** = `battle.grid.surface_h(p) + Islands.base_h()`. It needs
`battle`, which the shell has, but it is a `FieldView` method today.

⚠⚠ **A body is NOT drawn at `soldier_pos`, and a rectangle will show it.** `_put_walker` draws at
`Look.tile_point_px(at) + crowd + _body_offset_of(key)`:

| Term | How far it moves the body | Can the shell recompute it |
|---|---|---|
| `Look.crowd_offset_px(slot, Rules.TILE_CAPACITY)` | **`CROWD_SPREAD_RATIO` 0.30 × `TILE_PX` 40 = 12 world px = 0.30 조각**, and **exactly 0 for slot 0** — a lone body sits on its 조각 centre. ⚠ The cap passed in is **`TILE_CAPACITY` 3**, not 9 | **Yes** — the slot comes from `Grid.slot_of` |
| `_body_offset_of` = idle + knock + swing | **`_idle_offset` returns `Vector2.ZERO`**, so a standing body drifts nothing. A knocked body drifts `KNOCK_RATIO` 0.30 × drawn half-width for `KNOCK_SEC` 0.14 s; a swinging body up to **`SWING_REACH` 0.70 × drawn half-width** along its facing | ⚠⚠ **No.** It lives only in `FieldView._body` and the sim does not know it exists |

⇒ **A box drawn around a 검사 mid-swing can miss him by 0.70 of his own half-width.** ⚠ **The change

### What `_build_reach` costs when a box hands it nine

**It is ONE flood, not one per body.** Every picked body's 조각 is seeded into the same queue before
the BFS starts, so nine bodies cost nine extra `append`s.

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| **The board it floods** | `grid.w * grid.h` = **30 × 26 = 780 조각** on the real island |
| **Worst-case `Grid.can_step` calls** | 780 × 8 = **6,240**, then a 780-long `_standable` sweep and one `block_of` per lit 조각 |
| **How often it runs** | **Once, at `pick_many`, and never rebuilt** — `hand.gd` says so on `reach`: the moment anybody walks, the lit set is answering about a board that has moved on |
| **Cost of nine against cost of one** | **The same flood.** ⇒ the box does not multiply this |

**What the 부대 DOES multiply is the 이동선**, and it is already measured. `_process` calls
`_show_route(_pointer_at)` **every frame**, and `hand.gd`'s own header records, on this island with a
부대 of nine:

- **cache hit 0.04 ms** · **miss 3.9 ms** (nine paths sharing `Battle.field_to`, 0.43 ms each)
- **37.6 ms** if every body built its own `Grid.flow_field` at **3.72 ms** — **2.2 frames of a 60 Hz
  budget**

⚠⚠ **THE CHANGE MAP IS OUT OF DATE HERE AND THE CODE IS TRUE.** `.plan-round-two.json` step 24 says
`routes` calls `grid.flow_field(dest)` inside the per-body loop, never through `Battle.field_to`,
memoised by `if block == _route_block`. **`hand.gd` today calls `grid.path_from(battle.field_to(dest),
here, dest)` and keys the cache on `_route_seats` + `_route_starts`** — that was rewritten on
2026-09-01 with the numbers above. **The profiling step the map asks for is already done.**
