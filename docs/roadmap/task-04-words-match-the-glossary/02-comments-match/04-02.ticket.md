### The rule that decides each hit — three buckets, and only three

1. **A quotation of the user** — ⚠⚠ **never reworded.** Change the word and it is no longer a quotation
2. **A tombstone** — a kept number for a deleted thing. **Left, and it is not drift**
3. **Live prose** — the comment's own voice. **This is the only bucket that changes**

⚠ **Every hit is put in a bucket by reading it, one at a time.** A blanket replace is what this ticket
must not be.
