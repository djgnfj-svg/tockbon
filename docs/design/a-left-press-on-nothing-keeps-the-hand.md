# A left press on nothing keeps the hand

**Status**: ⚠⚠ **reversed by the user, 2026-09-02.** Ticket 03-04's Answer records the reversal, ticket
03-11 builds it, and `GLOSSARY.md`'s 뒤집힌 낱말 table carries it as its own row: 「ESC 아니면 부대를
안 놓는다」 → 「빈 땅을 왼쪽으로 누르면 놓는다」 (*"unless ESC, the 부대 is not let go"* → *"a left
press on empty ground lets go"*).
**This document is not deleted.** A reversed decision sits beside its reason so the same argument does
not have to be had twice.

## The rule this document rejects

**A left press on nothing keeps the hand, and ESC is the only way to let go.** While the hand holds a
부대, a press on ground the 부대 cannot be sent to — water, a dark 칸, off the board — changes nothing:
the 부대 stays picked, the reach stays lit, and only ESC empties the hand.

It was the user's own sentence:

> The user, 2026-08-31: ***"unless I press ESC, the move should stay the priority."***
> (「이게 esc를 하지 않는 이상 이동 우선으로 해줘야할듯한데」)

and it was written into the shell as **"A PRESS THAT CANNOT BE A WALK KEEPS THE HAND, AND ESC IS THE
ONLY WAY TO LET GO"**, in the function a left press on the island lands in.

## Why the rule existed

**One button did both jobs.** Until 2026-09-02 the left button picked a body with an empty hand and
ordered the walk with a full one. A press aimed a little wide — a pixel past the lit 칸, onto water —
could not be a walk, and if the shell had let go there, the player lost the 부대 they had just picked
and had to pick it again to try the same order twice. **Keeping the hand was the cheaper mistake**, and
the sentence above was the user asking for it after feeling the other one on screen.

## Why the reason is spent

**The walk moved to the right button** (ticket 03-11, 2026-09-02, read from the user's StarCraft
reference: the move order goes out on the right button's DOWN edge). With two buttons a wide left press
costs nothing — the left button never orders, so there is no order to lose by letting go — and the
rule was protecting a mistake the player can no longer make.

**StarCraft, which the user named, clears the selection on a click on nothing.** That is the rule now,
on the left button alone: a press on any point no body is drawn over empties the hand. ⚠ **One rule
and no special case for the sea** — lit ground, dark ground, water and off the board are all
「nothing」, because a hand kept only over water would be one more rule nobody asked for.

## What survives

- **ESC still lets go.** It is the second way, not a deleted one.
- **A RIGHT press on a 칸 the hand cannot reach keeps the hand.** The 2026-08-31 rule lives on there,
  on the button the order moved to — a right press that orders nobody changes nothing, and that half
  was not re-decided by the reversal (03-11, Out of scope).
- **The old line stays in the shell**, written onto with the reversal and dated, never deleted.
