# press — **the angles the user cannot ask for**

**The user is new to making games.** ⇒ **They cannot ask for the fork they have never seen.**
*"So this too, or that too?"* is the shape they asked for, and only somebody who has looked at how this is
normally done can produce it.

## ⚠⚠ Not `grilling`, and the difference is the point

| | Works on | Fails by |
|---|---|---|
| **`grilling`** | **What is already on the table** — a tree of what the user named | Re-asking what they settled |
| **`press`** | **What is NOT on the table** — the forks this direction forces | Never running, so a fork surfaces a month late |

## The method — **look first, then ask**

⚠⚠ **A question invented from your own head is worth nothing here.** It reproduces what the user already
thought of, which is the one thing they do not need.

1. **Send `research`** — what does everyone building this have to decide here, and **what do they disagree
   about?** That disagreement is the question.
2. **Send `lookup`** — what has this repo already settled, tried, or reversed? **Quote their old answer
   instead of asking again.**
3. **Subtract.** What is left — forced, unsettled, unasked — is the round.

**Two to five questions. Never more.**

## The shape — **the user's own, given 2026-08-24**

⚠ **The labels stay Korean**: they are printed to the user, who reads Korean.

```
---
**질문 1** : <one line>
**추천** : <one line>
**왜** : <one line>
```

- **One line each.** Reasoning that does not fit goes in the body above
- ⚠⚠ **Every question carries a recommendation.** A bare question hands the work back to the one person
  here who cannot answer it from experience
- ⚠ **No "the case against" under it** — killed 2026-08-22. **When the case against matters, the fork IS
  the question**

## When it runs

**`roadmap`** (the map is being set or repaired) · **`compass`** (the week's work is being chosen) ·
**`wrap-up`** (work is being split into tickets).

⚠⚠ **Never on an ordinary reply.** The every-reply question round was killed on 2026-08-27 for exactly
that.

## After

**Their answers are the decision, and `wrap-up` writes them** — never during the conversation.
⚠ **A question they did not answer is not a silent yes.** Carry it to the next round or into the ticket.
