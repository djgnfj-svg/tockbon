# 위에서 보이지 않는 세 부위

## Question

**머리 · 가슴 · 배 do not read on a top-down body. Do they get a protruding form, or are they invisible
upgrades on a game whose every state change is supposed to show?**

## The measurement this stands on

***"From above, only what sticks out reads. A top-down lion is an orange square — a mane is surface, and
surface does not show from above."*** That is a measured failure from the second dead game, and it is the
same measurement that **withdrew the paperdoll layout** (row 68) and gave the board its 3×2 grid.

**팔 · 손 · 다리 protrude and read. 머리 · 가슴 · 배 are surface.** Three of six.

## Why a dashboard does not settle it

Row 71 decided the refit screen **shows a slot's whole numbers at once**, and the record already says that is
**not decoration** — it is what makes fitting the three surface parts visible **at all**. ⚠ **But a number
changing on a refit screen is not the body changing on the field**, and 「내 병사를 만든다」 (row 64) is a
thing the user asked to settle **by looking, not by prose**.

## What stands

- ✅ **개념 C — a board of cells, the body as the result** (row 65), accepted **by looking**. The user's own
  reason was forward-looking: the glyphs are simple today and C is the frame that holds richer ones later
- ✅ **몸은 코드가 그리는 선** — a rounded square outline with one dot in the middle. Swapping in artwork is
  left open for later
- **This is why it is a prototype ticket and not a grilling ticket**: row 64 is a standing instruction that
  this concept is decided by a mockup, and the refit screen has been designed in words twice and **never
  looked at**

## What to make

The six-part body at the size it is actually drawn at, with the three surface parts fitted and unfitted, so
the difference is either visible or measurably not. **Link the artefact from this ticket; do not paste it.**

⚠ **A constant is not the screen.** The viewport is stretched 1.5× and camera zoom multiplies on top —
this repo got that wrong four times in one game. **Screenshot it, do not compute it.**
